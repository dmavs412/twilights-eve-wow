The Stockade

Spec #575. Vanilla's jail, map 34, two dungeons with the City Watch between them. One long hall of cells with a round room at each end, crossed near its middle by the corridor from the canal door. North is up. The corridor is the Watch's and is open ground; the east wing is the riot (30 to 34) and the west wing is the quiet wing (36 to 40), each a Phased Dungeon from the head of its stairs.

The Watch's ground

The gate hall, the corridor and its cells, open world on map 34. Warden Thelwater stands at the crossing and gives the quests. Quartermaster Mae Tollin repairs in the gate hall. Sergeant Hale stands across the head of the west stairs and speaks the Keep's refusal. Watchmen hold the east stairs, the door and the corridor's end. Convicts behind the corridor's bars recognise a notorious player (spec #558).

The riot east wing, 30 to 34

  1. Down the east stairs past the Portal. Two Rioters and a Ringleader at the foot (Quell the Riot).
  2. Row A, the near half of the cells. Three cells of two Rioters; a Ringleader and a Rioter at the lever post. Pull Lever A to drop the row's bars, or leave them up and Cutter calls two more out every twenty seconds.
  3. Varl Cutter where the rows meet, with two Ringleaders (Varl Cutter).
  4. Row B, the far half, the same again with Lever B.
  5. The round room at the wing's end. Captain Rook and two Bought Watchmen stand between you and the cell where Bertram Ash has barred himself in (The Turnkey). Ash gives The Captain's Keys once Rook is dead.
1 · Level 32 elite

Varl Cutter

The Defias ringleader who took Rook's purse, where row A meets row B, with two Ringleaders.

Calls two Rioters from each unlocked row every 20 s. Shiv, a bleed every 12 s. Fire Bottle every 20 s: a 4 yd fire patch for 10 s under a random character, with a warning. Drops the purse of Twilight coin.

2 · Level 34 · Boss Bar

Captain Ansel Rook

The Watch's captain, in the round room at the wing's end with two Bought Watchmen, in front of Ash's cell.

Shield Wall every 30 s: 60% less damage taken and 30% less dealt for 8 s. Captain's Cut at 200% every 10 s. Hamstring, a 50% snare for 6 s, every 15 s. Drops Rook's Keyring and Rook's Letters.

The quiet wing west wing, 36 to 40

  1. The sealed door at the head of the west stairs opens once for a character carrying Rook's Key (The Quiet Wing). Two Writ Warders at the foot of the stairs.
  2. The writ cells, the first four past the stairs: Wender Cogg, Hesper Lowe, Dorrin Vask and Tobin Marsh. Two patrol pairs, a Writ Warder and a Twilight Listener each, walk the hall between them.
  3. The scribe's room, a cell halfway along. Sister Quill and two Listeners (The Scribe, from Marsh).
  4. The lower gallery, the wing's far half. Three packs of three Hushed. They make no sound.
  5. The drain, the round room at the wing's end. The Hush. The grate in the floor offers The Drain once Quill is dead.
3 · Level 38 elite

Sister Quill

The Keep's scribe, in the scribe's room with two Twilight Listeners, writing down what each prisoner knows.

Take Down, a 4 s silence on a random character every 15 s. Ink every 25 s, a 2 s cast with a warning, then nobody within 8 yd of her can move for 3 s. Shadow Bolt. Drops the Book of Takings.

4 · Level 40 · Boss Bar

The Hush

A Twilight Silencer who came up the drain with the names, in the round room at the wing's end.

Hush, a 5 s silence every 18 s. Fear every 30 s. Shadow Bolt. Stillness every 40 s, a shield that soaks damage for 10 s. Drops the Writ of Names.

The walls are a schematic of the jail's shape, not a measured plan; the grey rings are the 93 vanilla spawn points, which are measured. The Build lane fits every footprint box, room, lever and spawn to the real walls (tickets #576, #578 and #581), so a marker here names a room, not a coordinate.
