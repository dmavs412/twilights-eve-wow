The Stockade

Spec #575. Vanilla's jail, map 34, levels 30 to 34. One long hall of cells with a round room at each end, crossed near its middle by the corridor from the canal door. North is up. The east wing of the hall is the Defias block and the west wing is the quiet wing. Warden Thelwater stands outside at the city door and never appears on this map.

Boss, numbered in walking order Friendly: Ott and the four witnesses Lever, portcullis, chest, grate Rioter packs A vanilla spawn point

The route

  1. The gate hall and the corridor. Rioters in three packs; Sergeant Ott at the corridor's north end with his back to the wall (Quell the Riot, Guards Below).
  2. Down the east stairs into the Defias block. A Ringleader and a Rioter guard each lever. Pull Lever A and Lever B to drop the bars on rows A and B, or fight Red Dace with two more rioters every twenty seconds. Dace holds the round room at the wing's end (Red Dace).
  3. Back up and down the west stairs. Bull Marrow is breaking the quiet wing's gate. Kill him, pull the gate's lever and the bars grind up (The Quiet Wing).
  4. The quiet wing. Four writ cells just past the gate, four witnesses, no enemies. The warder's room halfway along holds the evidence chest and the lower doors, which Pell left open (What They Saw, The Last of Westfall).
  5. Through the lower doors into the lower gallery, the wing's far half. Warder Pell and his two bought guards, counting the purse (Warder Pell).
  6. The drain, the round room at the wing's end. The Hush waits for the ogre to finish the doors. The grate in the floor offers The Drain once Pell is dead.

The bosses

1 · Level 31 elite

Red Dace

The Defias captain who took the deal, in the round room at the east wing's end with two Ringleaders.

Every 20 s he calls two Rioters out of each row whose bars are still up. Lock both rows and he fights alone. Drops his purse of Twilight coin.

2 · Level 32 elite

Bull Marrow

The ogre the Defias kept for breaking doors, at the foot of the west stairs against the quiet wing's gate.

Beam Swing, 250% of a swing every 8 s. Shove, a 6 yd nova with a two second warning. Health doubled. The gate's lever works once he is dead.

3 · Level 33 elite

Warder Pell

The turned guard, in the west wing's far half beyond the warder's room, with two Bought Guards.

Shield Wall every 25 s: half damage taken for 8 s. Fights to the death. Drops the Sealed Ledger.

4 · Level 34 · Boss Bar

The Hush

A Twilight Silencer who came up the drain with the names, in the round room at the west wing's end.

Hush, a 5 s silence every 18 s. Fear every 30 s. Shadow Bolt. Drops the Writ of Names.
