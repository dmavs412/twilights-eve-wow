The Stockade

Spec #575. Vanilla's jail, map 34, levels 30 to 34. North is up: the corridor runs north from the canal door, the Defias block is the east wing and the quiet wing is the west wing. Warden Thelwater stands outside at the city door and never appears on this map.

Boss, numbered in walking order Friendly: Ott and the four witnesses Lever, portcullis, chest, grate Rioter packs A vanilla spawn point (the walls are fitted to these)

The route

  1. The gate hall and the corridor. Rioters in three packs; Sergeant Ott at the corridor's end with his back to the wall (Quell the Riot, Guards Below).
  2. Down the east stairs into the Defias block. A Ringleader and a Rioter guard each lever. Pull Lever A and Lever B to drop the bars on rows A and B, or fight Red Dace with two more rioters every twenty seconds (Red Dace).
  3. The deep cells past Dace hold the rest of the riot and nothing the quests need.
  4. Back up and down the west stairs. Bull Marrow is breaking the quiet wing's gate. Kill him, pull the gate's lever and the bars grind up (The Quiet Wing).
  5. The quiet wing. Four writ cells, four witnesses, no enemies. The warder's room at the north end holds the evidence chest and the lower doors, which Pell left open (What They Saw, The Last of Westfall).
  6. Through the lower doors into the lower gallery. Warder Pell and his two bought guards, counting the purse (Warder Pell).
  7. The drain, the last room. The Hush waits for the ogre to finish the doors. The grate in the floor offers The Drain once Pell is dead.

The bosses

1 · Level 31 elite

Red Dace

The Defias captain who took the deal, at the far side of the east hall with two Ringleaders.

Every 20 s he calls two Rioters out of each row whose bars are still up. Lock both rows and he fights alone. Drops his purse of Twilight coin.

2 · Level 32 elite

Bull Marrow

The ogre the Defias kept for breaking doors, at the foot of the west stairs against the quiet wing's gate.

Beam Swing, 250% of a swing every 8 s. Shove, a 6 yd nova with a two second warning. Health doubled. The gate's lever works once he is dead.

3 · Level 33 elite

Warder Pell

The turned guard, in the lower gallery beyond the warder's room, with two Bought Guards.

Shield Wall every 25 s: half damage taken for 8 s. Fights to the death. Drops the Sealed Ledger.

4 · Level 34 · Boss Bar

The Hush

A Twilight Silencer who came up the drain with the names, in the last room past the gallery.

Hush, a 5 s silence every 18 s. Fear every 30 s. Shadow Bolt. Drops the Writ of Names.

Positions are the spec's reading. The Build lane fits every room, lever and spawn to the jail's walls from the 93 vanilla spawn points shown in grey (ticket #578), so a marker here is a room, not a coordinate.
