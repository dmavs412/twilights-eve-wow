# Elwynn Forest 10–30: Quest Design (Northshire format)

Follows the Northshire Abbey quest setup (94001–94009). Three quest types per sub-zone:

- **Story quests** — once per character, carry the plot. XP only unless noted.
- **Bounties** — repeatable as soon as handed in; only takeable inside the level range; pay half a level's XP flat across the range, 1 Token, and copper. Copper amounts are placeholders scaled from Northshire's 50–120.
- **Dungeon repeatables** — 10 drops: 35% from trash, 100% from named cult mobs, 3 from the boss. Available from 4 levels below the dungeon to 4 above.

**Items:** one ITEM quest per outdoor sub-zone (usually the elite kill) and one on each dungeon boss, a choice of one piece per fighter kind like 900101–900104. Hogger is the big one.

**Story spine:** Northshire established that the Twilight's Hammer paid kobolds to dig toward the Abbey and the Abbot let the cult in. The rest of Elwynn asks what they are digging for. In order the player learns: the cult funds the Defias to keep roads clear (Farmlands); it took Jasperlode from the kobolds to dig there (Fargodeep, Jasperlode); it moves "tribute" by water and has a mage tower ready (Stone Cairn, Azora); it holds Ridgepoint openly and digs a pit under Jerod's Landing (Eastvale, Landing); Hogger's camp is its staging ground on the new moon (Westbrook, Hogger). Marshal Dughan is the skeptic slowly convinced. The robed buyer behind all of it is Morganth, the shadow mage in the Tower of Ilgalar, so the arc ends with Dughan's letter to Lakeshire and Theocritus's grudge pointing the player into Redridge.

## Goldshire (10–13)

Goldshire is the hub. Its story quests send the player to talk, not kill: Marshal Dughan does not believe McBride's cult story yet.

### Story quests (once per character)

1. **94101 McBride's Report: Level 10.** Requires 94004 (The Abbot's Fall). McBride gives it. Task: carry the sealed report to Marshal Dughan in Goldshire. Story: McBride writes down what happened in the Abbey; Dughan is the one who has to act on it. Reward: XP only.
2. **94102 The Marshal's Doubt: Level 11.** Requires 94101. Dughan gives it. Task: ask Innkeeper Farley, Smith Argus and Remy "Two Times" whether anyone in Goldshire has seen the hammer-and-black-sun mark. Story: Dughan will not raise an alarm over one idol. Farley saw the mark on a crate from a wrecked cart; Argus has had strangers buying ore in bulk; Remy's biggest debtor skipped town the night the Abbey fell. Reward: XP only.
3. **94103 The Wrecked Cart: Level 12.** Requires 94102. Farley gives it. Task: recover the Sealed Strongbox from the Defias Highwayman at the wrecked cart on the Stormwind road. Story: the strongbox holds Twilight coin and a letter addressed to "the Collector, Brackwell." Dughan still calls it a Defias matter, but he sends you south. Reward: 4 silver.

## The Farmlands (11–14)

Stonefield, Maclure and Brackwell. The feud is a setup: Morgan the Collector, the moneylender at Brackwell manor, pays the Defias in Twilight coin to keep the farmers fighting so nobody watches the road to Jerod's Landing. Morgan is this sub-zone's Padfoot.

### Story quests (once per character)

1. **94201 The Collector's Letter: Level 12.** Requires 94103. Dughan gives it. Task: show the letter to Guard Thomas at the Stonefield–Maclure crossroads. Story: Thomas knows the name: Morgan has been buying up both families' debt for a year. Reward: XP only.
2. **94202 Princess: Level 12.** Requires nothing before it. Ma Stonefield gives it. Task: kill Princess, the elite boar wrecking the Maclure vineyard, and bring back her collar. Story: the collar is stamped with Morgan's mark. The pig was a gift to the Maclures, then let loose on Stonefield land. Reward: 3 silver. Optional ITEM: a small trinket; Princess is the first elite after Padfoot.
3. **94203 Mend the Fence: Level 13.** Requires 94201 and 94202. Guard Thomas gives it. Task: show the collar to Ma Stonefield and Joshua Maclure, then bring both to Thomas. Story: the feud was bought. Both farms open as vendors. Reward: XP only.
4. **94204 Morgan the Collector: Level 14.** Requires 94203. Dughan gives it. Task: kill Morgan the Collector, elite, in Brackwell manor, and bring his ledger. Story: the ledger lists Defias wages paid in Twilight coin, and "road to the Landing: keep clear." Reward: 8 silver, plus a choice of one of Morgan's items, one for each kind of fighter. **ITEM.**

## Crystal Lake & Fargodeep (13–16)

Fargodeep is the outdoor kobold warren that teaches Jasperlode. Crystal Lake has the murlocs. The sub-zone explains why the kobolds are here: the cult drove them out of Jasperlode.

### Story quests (once per character)

1. **94301 Narg's Orders: Level 14.** Requires 94204. Dughan gives it. Task: kill Narg the Taskmaster, elite, at the bottom of Fargodeep and bring his journal. Story: Morgan's ledger listed ore from Fargodeep. Narg's journal says the kobolds fled Jasperlode because "the ones who dig down, not across" took it from them. Reward: 6 silver, plus a choice of one of Narg's items. **ITEM.**
2. **94302 The Dead Courier: Level 14.** Requires nothing before it. Starts from the Courier's Satchel on the dead gryphon at the lake's north shore. Task: bring the satchel to Marshal Dughan. Story: inside is a Stormwind dispatch: cult activity confirmed in Redridge, Elwynn considered clear. Dughan now knows Stormwind is wrong. Reward: XP only.
3. **94303 The Keystone: Level 15.** Requires 94301. Foreman Gorn at the Fargodeep entrance gives it. Task: recover the rune-marked keystone from Gllrg the Tidecaller, the named murloc at Crystal Lake. Story: the murlocs looted the sealed Jasperlode entrance; the keystone opens its inner door. Reward: XP only. Opens Jasperlode.

## Jasperlode Mine dungeon (15–18)

The kobolds still inside are the ones who would not leave, and they are more afraid of what the cult woke at the bottom than of you. "Deep Overseer" is a placeholder boss name.

### Story quests (once per character)

1. **94401 Into Jasperlode: Level 15.** Requires 94303. Dughan gives it. Task: open the sealed door with the keystone and find Chieftain Grik's camp inside. Story: the mine is split: kobolds above, cult diggers below. Reward: XP only.
2. **94402 The Kobold King's Plea: Level 17.** Requires 94401. Chieftain Grik gives it and takes it back. Task: kill the Deep Overseer at the bottom of the mine. Story: Grik swears his people will leave Elwynn if the thing below dies. Players can honor that or wipe the camp on the way out. Reward: 10 silver, plus a choice of one of the Overseer's items. **ITEM.**
3. **94403 What Narg Feared: Level 18.** Requires 94402. Dughan gives it. Task: bring Dughan the Overseer's orders. Story: the orders carry the same mark as the idol from Echo Ridge, in the same hand. Dughan stops calling it a Defias matter. Reward: XP only.

### Dungeon repeatable

**94404 Resonant Ore: Level 17, available at levels 13–22.** Giver: Smith Argus in Goldshire. No earlier quest is needed. Task: bring back 10 Resonant Ore. Drop rates: Deep Kobolds 35%, Twilight Diggers always, the Deep Overseer always drops three. Reward: 4 silver and 3 Tokens.

## Stone Cairn Lake & Heroes' Vigil (16–19)

The quiet stretch between the two mid-level dungeons. First cultists seen in the open; first Riverpaw gnolls this far east.

### Story quests (once per character)

1. **94501 The Vigil Defaced: Level 16.** Requires nothing before it. Grave-tender Ilsa at Heroes' Vigil gives it. Task: clean 5 cairn stones of the painted mark, then kill the 6 Twilight Daubers who come back at dusk. Story: low-level cultists, and they run when hit. The mark is the one from Echo Ridge. Reward: XP only.
2. **94502 Rites at the Stone: Level 18.** Requires 94501 and 94403. Ilsa gives it. Task: hold the cairn through one night against three waves of cultists, then kill Ritualist Serane, elite, who leads the last one. Story: Serane's orders read "the tower is ready; the tribute moves by water." Reward: 8 silver, plus a choice of one of Serane's items. **ITEM.**
3. **94503 An Echo in the Ore: Level 19.** Requires 94502. Starts from the Resonant Shard that Serane drops. Task: bring the shard to Toren, Theocritus's apprentice, at the base of the Tower of Azora. Story: Toren recognizes it from the tower's experiments and goes pale. Reward: XP only. Opens Azora.

## Tower of Azora dungeon (19–22)

**Morganth's Reach.** The robed buyer was Morganth, the shadow mage who holds the Tower of Ilgalar in Redridge and has been Theocritus's rival for years. He bought Toren's Resonant Ore readings, used them to pry Azora's wards loose, and now the tower's portals open wherever Morganth has been probing. Theocritus is bound in the top chamber by a projection of him. The dungeon is Azora's floors plus four portal stops in the existing world, so no new terrain.

| Floor | Ward | Portal stop (existing Classic location) | Mobs | Mini-boss |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Water | The shallows of Lake Everstill, Redridge | Shadowhide Gnolls, Shadowhide Darkweavers | Shadowhide Warlock "Gresh the Drowned" |
| 2 | Earth | The grounds below the Tower of Ilgalar | Shadowhide Brutes, Shadowhide Slayers | Shadowhide captain "Rokar Ilgalar's-Hand" |
| 3 | Fire | The Blackrock foothills on Redridge's northern edge | Twilight Acolytes, Twilight Geolord, Lesser Voidwalkers | Twilight Geolord "Speaker Thane" (the cult and Morganth are allied) |
| 4 | Shadow | The cellar beneath Ilgalar | Voidwalkers, Shadowhide Darkweavers | Voidwalker "the Kept" guarding Morganth's ore cache |
| Top | Master portal | Azora's top chamber | Shadowhide Gnolls pour through the portal on a timer | **Shadow of Morganth** (Morganth's model with a shadow aura) with Theocritus bound beside it |

Each ward stone sits at the portal stop; fixing it pins that portal and sends the party back to Azora. Gnolls at every stop are the point: the player has fought Riverpaw since Stone Cairn, and Shadowhide gnolls say the same master is pulling both packs. Hogger's "promised power" came through Morganth.

### Story quests (once per character)

1. **94601 The Tower Is Open: Level 19.** Requires 94503. Toren gives it. Task: enter the tower and re-attune the 4 ward stones, one through each floor's portal. Story: each ward you fix pins one portal in place. Toren admits Theocritus has not been seen in a week, and that the portals "keep opening on Redridge." Reward: XP only.
2. **94602 The Apprentice's Secret: Level 21.** Requires 94601. Starts from Toren's Sale Note, found on the third floor. Task: confront Toren at the tower base. Story: the buyer signed as "M., Ilgalar." Toren knew exactly who that was. Hand him to Dughan or let him run; if he runs he turns up at Ridgepoint (94704). Reward: XP only.
3. **94603 Theocritus Unbound: Level 22.** Requires 94601. Theocritus gives it from the top chamber. Task: kill the Shadow of Morganth and close the master portal. Story: the projection breaks and the tower is Theocritus's again. He is furious in a way the player has not seen from him. Reward: 12 silver, plus a choice of one of the Shadow's items, and a one-use Azora teleport scroll. **ITEM.**
4. **94604 Where It Pointed: Level 22.** Requires 94603. Theocritus gives it. Task: tell Dughan what the portals showed. Story: the cult's people went to Ridgepoint Tower on the Eastvale ridge; Morganth himself never left Ilgalar. Dughan writes a letter for Supervisor Raelen and tells Theocritus that Redridge is not his jurisdiction. Theocritus does not agree. Reward: XP only.

### Dungeon repeatable

**94605 Ward Shards: Level 21, available at levels 17–26.** Giver: Theocritus (after 94603) or Toren before that. No earlier quest is needed. Task: bring back 10 Ward Shards. Drop rates: Shadowhide Gnolls 35%, Twilight Portalists and Voidwalkers always, the Shadow of Morganth always drops three. Reward: 5 silver and 3 Tokens.

## Eastvale & Ridgepoint Tower (20–24)

## Mirror Lake & Jerod's Landing (23–26)

Jerod's Landing is the cult's supply line out of Elwynn, and the pit under it is their second dig. Mirror Lake is the approach, held by Riverpaw gnolls working beside the Defias.

### Story quests (once per character)

1. **94801 Remy's Last Debtor: Level 23.** Requires 94102. Remy "Two Times" gives it. Task: find Dobbs, the debtor who skipped town, at Jerod's Landing. Story: Dobbs keeps the smugglers' ledger now. He trades the shipping schedule for his life and Remy's forgiveness. Reward: XP only.

4. **94804 Burn the Boats: Level 26.** Requires 94803. Keane gives it. Task: torch the 3 smuggler skiffs before they go upriver toward Lake Everstill. Story: the supply line is cut and the finale is a race to the new moon. Reward: 5 silver. Opens Westbrook.

The staging base for the night raid. Every story quest hands the player something used inside Hogger's camp: a horn, a sketch map, the sentry rotation, poisoned meat.

### Story quests (once per character)

Story: the horn is how Hogger's camp calls its gnolls to a gate. Reward: 8 silver, plus a choice of one of Ragtooth's items. **ITEM.** The horn is a separate quest item kept for the raid.

## Hogger's Camp dungeon (28–30)

Hogger was promised power by the cult in exchange for digging under his camp, and tonight they move what they found. The Westbrook items pay off inside: the horn pulls gnolls off a gate, the Tainted Meat quiets the kennels, the sketch map and sentry rotation give the route.

### Story quests (once per character)

1. **95001 Into the Camp: Level 28.** Requires 94906. Commander Vance gives it. Task: get inside Hogger's camp after dark and reach the Twilight tent. Story: the whole camp is awake and waiting for the new moon. Reward: XP only.
2. **95002 The Tribute: Level 29.** Requires 95001. Dughan's letter, carried from 94906, gives it. Task: take the sealed Tribute crate from the Twilight tent before it leaves. Story: the crate is marked for the Tower of Ilgalar; what is inside is the hook into Redridge. Reward: XP only.
3. **95003 Free the Prisoners: Level 29.** Requires nothing before it. Starts from Scout Hale, caged in the camp. Task: free the 4 captured Westbrook scouts and Eastvale lumberjacks. Story: optional; the freed NPCs turn up at the garrison afterward. Reward: 5 silver.
4. **95004 Hogger: Level 30.** Requires 95001. Vance gives it. Task: kill Hogger, elite. Story: the zone's capstone. If the Stockades exist, capture is the alternate turn-in. Reward: 20 silver, plus a choice of one of Hogger's items, one for each kind of fighter. **ITEM: the big one.**
5. **95005 Marshal Dughan's Report: Level 30.** Requires 95002 and 95004. Dughan gives it in Goldshire. Task: return with the Tribute and word of Hogger. Story: Dughan writes to Magistrate Solomon in Lakeshire; the exit quests carry it. Reward: 10 silver and the zone title.

### Dungeon repeatable

**95006 Twilight Relics of the Camp: Level 29, available at levels 25–35.** Giver: Commander Vance. No earlier quest is needed. Task: bring back 10 Twilight Relics. Drop rates: Riverpaw Cultists 35%, Twilight Zealots always, Hogger always drops three. Reward: 8 silver and 3 Tokens.

## Exit quests into Redridge (30)

Three short quests that hand the player to Lakeshire the way 94101 handed them to Goldshire. Two voices send them: Dughan, officially, and Theocritus, personally. Nothing in Redridge itself is designed here.

### Story quests (once per character)

1. **95101 A Letter for Lakeshire: Level 30.** Requires 95005. Dughan gives it. Task: carry Dughan's letter over the Lakeridge Highway to Magistrate Solomon in Lakeshire. Story: Dughan has written down everything from the Abbey to Hogger's camp. Solomon reads it and says Redridge has worse problems than a cult, and he means the Blackrock orcs. Reward: XP only.
2. **95102 Theocritus's Grudge: Level 30.** Requires 94604 and 95005. Theocritus gives it. Task: take his sealed challenge to the foot of the Tower of Ilgalar and leave it on the door. Story: Theocritus will not let Morganth keep what he stole. Shadowhide gnolls watch the player do it and do not attack; Morganth wants it delivered. Reward: XP only.
3. **95103 What the Tribute Was For: Level 30.** Requires 95002 and 95102. Starts from the Tribute crate once Theocritus has examined it. Task: bring Theocritus's findings to Magistrate Solomon. Story: whatever is in the crate, Morganth was paying the cult to dig it up under Elwynn and float it up the river to Everstill. Solomon finally takes the cult seriously; the Redridge arc opens from here. Reward: 10 silver.

If you want a fourth beat, Toren is the natural one: if he was freed at Ridgepoint (94704), he is in Lakeshire's inn, and Morganth has already found him.
