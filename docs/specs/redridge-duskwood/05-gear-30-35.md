blues for its one named-elite item quest, 98107 "The Thresher".

## Quest Lines (the same in every gear ticket of this arc)

- **What they are.** A Line is one item for the icon rule (ADR 0026): its rows repeat band after
  band with only their numbers changing and share one icon, as the `world-*` Lines do. Quest
  rewards now come in Lines too, so the whole arc needs 36 new icons, not one per item:
  - **20 green Quest Lines**, the World shape (`gear.csv` `world-chest-cloth` to
    `world-ring-mind-b`, entries 902000 to 902019 show it): `quest-chest-cloth`,
    `quest-chest-leather`, `quest-chest-mail`, `quest-chest-plate`, `quest-sword`,
    `quest-shield`, `quest-dagger`, `quest-bow`, `quest-staff`, `quest-mace`, `quest-held`,
    `quest-amulet-might`, `quest-amulet-swift`, `quest-amulet-mind`, `quest-ring-might-a`,
    `quest-ring-might-b`, `quest-ring-swift-a`, `quest-ring-swift-b`, `quest-ring-mind-a`,
    `quest-ring-mind-b`. Each band gets one row of each.
  - **16 blue Quest Lines** for named-elite item quests: `quest-elite-chest-cloth`,
    `quest-elite-chest-leather`, `quest-elite-chest-mail`, `quest-elite-chest-plate`,
    `quest-elite-sword`, `quest-elite-dagger`, `quest-elite-staff`, `quest-elite-shield`,
    `quest-elite-bow`, `quest-elite-held`, `quest-elite-amulet-might`, `quest-elite-amulet-swift`,
    `quest-elite-amulet-mind`, `quest-elite-ring-might`, `quest-elite-ring-swift`,
    `quest-elite-ring-mind`. Each gets rows only where an elite quest uses it.
- **Icons.** The first row of a Line picks its icon; every later row of that Line copies it. All
  36 are stock icons `python3.15 -I tools/twilights-eve/icon_check.py` accepts, none painted.
  Check by icon name, not display id. The supply (`docs/specs/prep/gear-31-60.md`, "Free stock
  icons"): rings are scarce (16 free; this arc takes 9), bows have one free icon (the two bow Lines
  may take crossbow or gun icons), staves 11 free (this arc takes 2), Chests 39 free (this arc
  takes 8). Where the sheet's `world-*` rows leave the `icon` column empty and take the display's
  icon, do the same; the Line must still end up with one icon that no other Line uses.
- **Rows.** Add each with `python3.15 tools/twilights-eve/sheet.py add-row`, never by hand-editing
  the CSV (CLAUDE.md), with its `line` cell set. Regenerate `crates/twilight-realm/content/gear.toml`
  with `python3.15 tools/twilights-eve/build_gear.py`.
- **Levels.** Every row of a band sits at the band's set level (the gear 31-60 draft's Loot Band
  levels) and requires the band's lowest quest level: band 30-35 item level 33, required 30;
  35-40 item level 38, required 36; 40-45 item level 43, required 40; 45-50 item level 48,
  required 45. All `bonding = Pickup`.
- **Budgets** (ADR 0026 as amended): `round_half_up(base x P(item level))`, base green 12, blue
  26, a two-hander's rounded budget doubled; P(L) = 3 x 1.03^(L-30) above 30. Weapon DPS is its
  anchor x P(L): green one-hander 12.6, staff 16.8, bow 9.6; blue 16.5, 21.8, 12.5.
  `build_gear.py` refuses a row more than 4 points or 10% of DPS off, and is the judge.

  | Item level | Green | Green 2H | Blue | Blue 2H | Green DPS 1H / staff / bow | Blue DPS 1H / staff / bow |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 33 | 39 | 78 | 85 | 170 | 41.3 / 55.1 / 31.5 | 54.1 / 71.5 / 41.0 |
  | 38 | 46 | 92 | 99 | 198 | 47.9 / 63.8 / 36.5 | 62.7 / 82.8 / 47.5 |
  | 43 | 53 | 106 | 115 | 230 | 55.5 / 74.0 / 42.3 | 72.7 / 96.0 / 55.1 |
  | 48 | 61 | 122 | 133 | 266 | 64.4 / 85.8 / 49.0 | 84.3 / 111.3 / 63.8 |

- **Flavours** (#448 decisions 5 and 6): Might spends 2/3 Strength, 1/3 Stamina; Swift 2/3 Agility,
  1/3 Stamina; Mind 1/2 Intellect, 1/4 Spirit, 1/4 Stamina, and a Mind weapon or held item spends
  half its points as Spell Power (2 per point), the rest on Intellect. Cloth is Mind, leather
  Swift, mail and plate Might; the sword and shield Might, the dagger and bow Swift, the staff,
  mace and held item Mind. The two ring Lines of a Flavour vary by up to 2 points so they read
  differently.
- **Armour** (#272 anchor): cloth 100, leather 175, mail 300, plate 375, x1.5 x P(L), a blue x1.15;
  a shield follows the sheet's nearest shield row scaled by P(L). The build does not check it.
- **The rest.** `sell_price` and `durability` copy a vanilla row of the same slot, quality and
  near level in the vmangos `item_template`; `display_id` from a vanilla item of the same type
  (ADR 0030 keeps model and icon apart). Each band's four green Chests share one Wardrobe set,
  one look per armour type from a low-level vanilla family no other row wears; the blue Chests
  have their own. Names are placeholders: one name stem per band for the greens, as the Riot
  set's stems are, and a stem per elite quest for the blues.
