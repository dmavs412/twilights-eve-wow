## The Grey Robes (decision 14)

Men in grey robes with a crowned mark, digging at Raven Hill at night. Players must be able to
tell them from the Twilight's Hammer: a different body and name, and no hammer-and-sun anywhere.
Nobody names their master.

Two templates in `content/mobs.toml`, after Watch Officer Harlan Pike (96047), in the Twilight
Cultist's form (96024, line 1503), each with a comment naming this ticket:

| Entry | Name | Rank | Class | Levels | Display | Faction | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 96048 | Grey Robe Digger | 0 | warrior | 37-38 | 2492 | 14 | melee, a shovel's look is fine |
| 96049 | Grey Robe Lantern-bearer | 0 | mage | 38 | 2492 | 14 | `spells = ["shadow_bolt_creature"]` |

Display 2492 is vmangos's Scarlet Abbot, a robed human, a placeholder ticket 03 lists in
`docs/art-assets.md`. Multipliers as the Twilight Cultist's.

form of Watch Officer Pike's (line 71): guids 961501 to 961512, each camp two Diggers and one
Lantern-bearer standing within 6 yd of its centre, `position` z written as 0.0 for
