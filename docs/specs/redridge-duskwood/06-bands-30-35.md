## The bands

below are each sub-zone's ADT chunks, rolled up through `AreaTable.dbc` for this spec; use them as
the starting box and trim so that no two bands' boxes overlap. A sub-zone may take two or three
boxes where one rectangle would swallow a neighbour. Add every box to the file's `bounds` too.
Put a comment above each band naming the anchors used, as `elwynn_bands.toml` does.
