# Gear from 31 to 60: grill prep

Draft for a grill, written 2026-11-07. It covers zone drops, quest rewards, dungeon loot and
dungeon chests for Redridge, Duskwood, Stormwind and the Burning Steppes, levels 31 to 60.
Every guess is marked "Assumed:". Nothing here is decided until Davis answers the grill.

## 1. What exists today

### The rules

- **Every Gear row is hand-authored** in `data/twilights-eve/gear.csv` (989 rows today, the
  Deeprun rows of #592 still uncommitted). `tools/twilights-eve/build_gear.py` writes
  `crates/twilight-realm/content/gear.toml`. Ten Gear Slots only (ADR 0007): Weapon, Offhand,
  Ranged, Ammo, two Rings, two Trinkets, Chest, Amulet.
- **Budgets follow the Power Curve** (ADR 0026 as amended, `content/power_curve.toml`).
  P(L) is L/10 up to 30, then 3% compounding. `budget = round_half_up(base x P(L))` with base
  white 4, green 12, blue 26, epic 36, a two-hander doubled. `build_gear.py` refuses a row from
  entry 900000 up whose points stray more than 4 from the budget, or whose weapon DPS strays
  more than 10% from its anchor x P(L). Its anchors (`WEAPON_DPS`): green one-hander 12.6,
  staff 16.8, bow 9.6; blue 16.5, 21.8, 12.5; epic 19.0, 25.3, 14.4; white 10.1, 13.4, 7.7.
- **Armour** is #272's level 10 anchor (cloth 100, leather 175, mail 300, plate 375) x1.5 x
  P(L), white x0.85, blue x1.15. The Deeprun and Stockade rows follow it. The build does not
  check armour.
- **Four Loot Sources.** White (drops only, ADR 0027), World (green), Quest (green), Dungeon
  (blue). Elite blues and epics sit on top.
- **Loot Bands** (`content/loot_bands.toml`): a kill on Eastern Kingdoms rolls 8% one white
  and 4% one green of the mob's band. A mob above the last band rolls the last band. Six bands
  exist: 1-10, 11-14, 15-18, 19-22, 23-26, 27-30. Adding a band is data only
  (`rules/loot.rs` reads the list). Elites add their own `rolls` in `mobs.toml`: 20% one Elite
  pool blue, then 50% one World green of their band.
- **A Line shares one icon** (ADR 0026), and no two Lines or unlined items share one. ADR 0030
  split the model (`display_id`) from the icon (`icon` column), so each row of a Line can wear
  its own model and Wardrobe. `tools/twilights-eve/icon_check.py` counts a Line as one item.
- **The Tier Step** (`mobs.toml` `[difficulty.tier_step]`): every mob from 31 up takes x1.5
  health and x1.5 damage. Gear does not take it (GLOSSARY). The Tier 2 Tier Bonus is meant to
  close the gap; the file calls 1.5 a placeholder.

## 2. Proposal

### Loot Bands 31 to 60

Six bands of five levels, each with 21 whites, 20 World greens and 6 Elite blues at its set
level: 47 rows a band, 282 rows. Every row extends an existing Line, so the bands cost no new
icon.

| Band | Levels | Set level | Where (Assumed: names come from the lore bible) | Green budget |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 31-35 | 33 | Lakeshire, Darkshire, the Stockade riot | 39 |
| 8 | 36-40 | 38 | Redridge canyons, Raven Hill, the quiet wing | 46 |
| 9 | 41-45 | 43 | Stonewatch, the Duskwood graves | 53 |
| 10 | 46-50 | 48 | Render's Valley, the Anchor's return | 61 |
| 11 | 51-55 | 53 | Burning Steppes west, the Altar | 71 |
| 12 | 56-60 | 58 | Burning Steppes east, Dreadmaul | 82 |

- Band numbers count Northshire as band 1, the order of `loot_bands.toml`.
- `required_level` is the band's first level, as Elwynn's.
- Each Chest of a band wears that band's Wardrobe: one look per armour type per band, 24 looks.
- Entries continue Elwynn's formulas: whites `901000 + 21 x (band - 2) + i` (band 7 starts at
  901105), greens `902000 + 20 x (band - 2) + i` (902100), Elite blues 904024 up, six a band.
- The Stockade and Deeprun trash pick up bands 7 and 8 with no change of their own.
- Elites in Redridge and Duskwood roll their band's Elite pool and greens, as Elwynn's do.
  Assumed: about 12 of the 30 elite templates are worth a roll; the rest are Blackrock grunts
  that a dungeon spec owns.

### Quest reward gear

- Every story quest pays a choice of three Quest greens at the quest's level, one per Flavour,
  in the slot cycle of #448 decision 9.
- Quest greens join **20 new Quest Lines** (`quest-*`, the World shape). That costs 20 icons
  once, where Elwynn's unlined rule would cost three icons a quest.
- The rows come with each zone's quest spec, not this one: Redridge and Duskwood have no
  quests yet. This spec reserves the 20 icons in a list and writes the rule.
- A quest that ends a dungeon pays that dungeon set's four Chests, as the Stockade does. No
  separate Capstone rows.
- Assumed: about 60 story quests from 31 to 60, so about 180 Quest green rows later.

### Dungeon loot

Each dungeon gets a set of 20 blues on the `dungeon-*` Lines at its top level, required level
three below it (the Stockade and Deeprun rule). Final boss 100% one of the set, stop bosses 50%,
elite trash 1%. The final boss also rolls 5% one of three Flavoured epics (#448 decision 13).

| Dungeon | Levels | Set item level | Required | Blue budget | Epics (5% on the final boss) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mistmantle Manor | 32-36 | 36 | 33 | 93 | three amulets, 36 |
| Tower of Ilgalar | 34-38 | 38 | 35 | 99 | three main hands, 38 (Morganth) |
| Raven Hill Deeps | 40-44 | 44 | 41 | 118 | three off hands, 44 |
| Stonewatch Keep | 45-48 | 48 | 45 | 133 | three main hands, 48 |
| Altar of Storms | 50-52 | 52 | 49 | 149 | three amulets, 52 |
| Blackrock Stronghold | 52-55 | 55 | 52 | 163 | three off hands, 55 |
| Dreadmaul Rock | 55-58 | 58 | 55 | 178 | three main hands, 58 |

- 140 blue rows from 905140, 20 per dungeon in the order above. 21 epics from 905909.
- The Stockade and Deeprun keep what they have. They gain no epics.
- Each dungeon's own spec wires the rolls to its bosses. This spec writes the pools into each
  dungeon's file. Assumed: a `[loot]` table with `set` and `epics` lists that the shells'
  parser tolerates or gains.
- Each dungeon gets one look per armour type, 28 looks. Assumed: from low-level vanilla
  families, as Davis wants many looks.

### Dungeon chests

One bonus chest per dungeon, earned by an optional challenge that the dungeon's own spec
designs (the Deeprun gold chest is the model). It holds one blue of the set and coin. The
chest is the `ObjectKind::Chest { items, money }` run-time object that #592 makes, so no new
realm code. Assumed: "dungeon chests" in the brief means chest objects in a dungeon, not the
Capstone Chests of #448.

### Set pieces

No item sets with bonuses. A set bonus needs ItemSet.dbc rows in the client patch and a new
realm component family, which is ADR territory. "Set" stays a name for a dungeon's 20 rows.

### Trinkets

Out of scope. Trinkets carry an effect, not stats (#458), so they get their own spec. With 104
free trinket icons they are the cheapest slot for a later pass.

### Icon budget

Against the free stock: rings 6 of 16, amulets 9 of 28, Chests 4 of 39, staves 4 of 11,
ranged 3 of 15 (bows have 1 free, so the ranged rows take crossbow or gun icons), every other
shape under a fifth of its supply. No painted icon. `docs/art-assets.md` gains nothing unless
a decision below changes this.

## 3. Open decisions, most important first

1. **Do Quest greens share icons in Lines?**
   - A. 20 new Quest Lines, 20 icons.
   - B. One icon per Quest green, the Elwynn rule: about 180 icons, so rings and staves run
     out and Davis paints about 100 PNGs.
   - C. Quest greens join the World Lines, no new icon, so a quest ring looks like a drop.

   Recommend A: B's icons do not exist, and C makes quest rewards look like trash drops.
2. **How wide is a Loot Band?**
   - A. Six bands of five levels.
   - B. Eight bands of four, as Elwynn (the last band 59-60).
   - C. Three bands by zone arc: 31-40, 41-50, 51-60.

   Recommend A: greens step 16% a band, close to Elwynn's feel, with 2 fewer bands to author
   than B.
3. **Does 31+ gear take any of the Tier Step?**
   - A. No. Gear stays on the Power Curve and the Tier 2 Tier Bonus closes the gap.
   - B. Gear from 31 takes x1.25.
   - C. Decide after a fight-model run.

   Recommend A with C's check: the glossary rule stands and the check ticket measures it.
4. **Are bands 11 and 12 (51-60) authored now?**
   - A. Now, named from the lore bible.
   - B. With the Burning Steppes spec.

   Recommend A: the Altar, Blackrock Stronghold and Dreadmaul sets need those levels anyway,
   and mobs above 50 would otherwise roll 46-50 forever.
5. **Do the seven dungeons drop epics?**
   - A. Three at 5% on each final boss, 21 icons.
   - B. None, as the Stockade and Deeprun.
   - C. Only Ilgalar (Morganth) and the three 50s dungeons, 12 icons.

   Recommend A: an epic lasts about 11 levels past 30, the hero's while #448 wanted.
6. **What does a dungeon-ending quest pay?**
   - A. The set's four Chests, the Stockade rule.
   - B. Four unique Capstone Chests, 28 Chest icons of 39.
   - C. A new Capstone Line, 4 icons.

   Recommend A: no icons and no rows, and the Stockade already works this way.
7. **Does every dungeon get a bonus chest?**
   - A. One per dungeon for an optional challenge, one set blue and coin.
   - B. None.
   - C. One always in the final room.

   Recommend A: the Deeprun gold chest proves the model, and the challenge belongs to each
   dungeon's spec.
8. **New looks, or copy Hogger's?**
   - A. One look per armour type per band and per dungeon, 52 looks.
   - B. Copy Hogger's camp, as the Stockade and Deeprun did.
   - C. Bands only, 24 looks.

   Recommend A: Davis wants many looks, and ADR 0030 made a look cost no icon.
9. **Do whites continue past 30?**
   - A. Yes, 21 a band.
   - B. No, greens are the floor.

   Recommend A: no icons and mechanical rows. Whites keep an armour-type floor for a new
   Calling after Advancement.
10. **Where do Redridge and Duskwood elites get their rolls?**
    - A. A `rolls` table by entry in `zones.toml`, a small realm change.
    - B. Shadow each template in `mobs.toml`, as Princess.

    Recommend A: B copies 20-line templates over generated files 12 times.
11. **Do the 26 vanilla rows at item level 31-42 stay?**
    - A. Retire them to `retired_items.toml`.
    - B. Leave them.

    Recommend B for now: nothing drops them, and a cleanup ticket can retire them later.
12. **Item sets with bonuses?**
    - A. None.
    - B. One set per 50s dungeon, a later spec with an ADR.

    Recommend A: it crosses the client/realm seam for little gain with ten slots.

## 4. Ticket sketch

Two lanes so two Build agents never edit the same file at once. Lane A owns
`data/twilights-eve/gear.csv` and the generated `gear.toml`, and takes its tickets one after
another. Lane B owns everything else.

### Tool tickets (lane B)

- **T1 tools: `sheet.py scale-line`.** Writes a Line's next row at a new entry and item level,
  scaling stats, DPS, armour, block and sell price by P(L), the name and look left for the
  author. A unit test in `tools/twilights-eve/tests`. Assumed useful: 422 rows that differ
  only in numbers. Files: `tools/twilights-eve/sheet.py`, its test, `docs/spell_pipeline.md`.
- **T2 tools: armour in the gate.** `build_gear.py` refuses a checked Chest or shield more than
  10% off the armour rule. Files: `build_gear.py`, its test.
- **T3 realm: elite rolls from `zones.toml`** (decision 10). Files: `src/mobs.rs`, a realm test.
- **T4 docs: GLOSSARY and reserved icons.** The Quest Line term, and the 20 reserved Quest Line
  icons in `docs/specs/` so other tickets skip them. Files: `GLOSSARY.md`, a spec page.

## Grill answers (Davis, 2026-11-08)

1. Quest greens: (A) Quest Lines that share one icon per slot (settled in the quest grill; the Redridge/Duskwood arc's gear tickets #600, #605, #610, #615 create them, with blue elite Lines too).
2. Band width: (A) five levels. But see 4: only 31-35, 36-40, 41-45, 46-50 now.
3. Tier Step: (A) gear stays on the Power Curve; the Tier 2 Tier Bonus closes the gap, measured by a fight-model check.
4. Bands 51-60: (B) NOT NOW; they come with the Burning Steppes content spec. Mobs above 50 roll the 46-50 band until then.
5. Epics: (A) three per final boss at 5% for the four 30-50 dungeons (Mistmantle, Ilgalar, Raven Hill Deeps, Stonewatch).
6. Dungeon-ending quests: (A) the set's four Chests, the Stockade rule (settled in the quest and dungeon grills).
7. Bonus chests: (A) one per dungeon for an optional challenge, one set blue and coin; the challenge per dungeon is this spec's Assumed reading for the four 30-50 dungeons.
8. Looks: (A) new looks everywhere (one per armour type per band and per dungeon set), AND looks progress with item level like WoW: a higher-level item uses a higher-level vanilla look. This replaces the old "looks come from low-level families" rule for new rows.
9. Whites: (B) NO whites past 30; greens are the floor.
10. Elite rolls (Claude): (A) a rolls table by entry in zones.toml.
11. Vanilla 31-42 rows (Claude): (B) leave them.
12. Set bonuses: (A) none now.
