# Sample rewrites for review

Eleven existing quests and one rumour's notes, rewritten to fit `docs/lore-bible.md`. Nothing in
the game changes yet. Mark each one keep, change or drop, and say why. The voice of the rest
follows what you keep.

Each sample keeps its quest's mobs, counts, items and chain. "Angle" names the person's story and
the one thread of the plot it carries, with its rung on the reveal ladder.

## 1. 94005 Wolves at the Fold

Eagan Peltskinner, Northshire, level 3 bounty. Kill 10 Young or Timber Wolves.

Angle: the wolves are thin strangers pushed in from the Westfall side, and Eagan's brother farmed
there. No plot thread; the shrinking world is the whole story.

- **Title:** Wolves at the Fold
- **Objectives:** Kill 10 Young Wolves or Timber Wolves in Northshire Valley, then return to Eagan
  Peltskinner.
- **Details:** More wolves every season, and they aren't Northshire wolves. They come in thin from
  the west, from the Westfall side, where my brother ran sheep before the Hush took it. Whatever's
  over there, they won't stay near it. So they come here, to my fold. I'll pay on every pack you
  thin. Young or timber, it makes no odds to me.
- **Progress:** Still hear them out there? I've stopped sleeping through the night.
- **Completion:** That buys the fold a few quiet nights. More will come. They've nowhere else to
  go, same as the rest of us. My coin will be here when they do.

## 2. 94006 Pelts for Peltskinner

Eagan Peltskinner, level 4 bounty. Collect 8 Northshire Wolf Pelts.

Angle: winter furs used to come down from Dun Morogh, and that road ends in grey now. No plot
thread.

- **Title:** Pelts for Peltskinner
- **Objectives:** Bring 8 Northshire Wolf Pelts to Eagan Peltskinner.
- **Details:** The quartermaster in Goldshire pays for every pelt I send, and he pays more each
  month. Furs used to come down from Dun Morogh. That road ends in grey now, and nobody's walked
  it back. So it's Elwynn wolves or a cold winter for the Watch. Bring me pelts, whole ones if you
  can.
- **Progress:** Any pelts for me?
- **Completion:** Good, thick hides. That's a few more men warm at the gate this winter. Here's
  your share.

## 3. 94101 McBride's Report

Marshal McBride to Marshal Dughan, level 10 story. Carry the sealed report.

Angle: McBride has sent riders to Stormwind and heard nothing back (missing news). Dughan
believes in Defias, not cults. Rung 1, "It's a Defias matter."

- **Title:** McBride's Report
- **Objectives:** Carry Marshal McBride's sealed report to Marshal Dughan in Goldshire.
- **Details:** It's all written down: the idol in the undercroft, the robes, the Abbot ringing the
  bell for them. I've sent three riders to Stormwind since the Abbey fell. Not one has come back
  with an answer. Dughan is closer, and he can't say he never got it. Put it in his hands and no
  one else's.
- **Progress:** Have you got the report for Marshal Dughan?
- **Completion:** McBride's seal. A cult, he says, and the Abbot ringing the bell for them. Brother
  Sammuel heard confessions in that Abbey for thirty years. I've had Defias in my fields since
  spring, and Defias I understand. I'll read every word. Don't ask me to believe all of it.

## 4. 94202 Princess

Ma Stonefield, level 12 story. Kill Princess and bring back her collar.

Angle: land is tight since the Westfall families crossed the ridge, and the old feud with the
Maclures has teeth now. Keeps the existing thread: the collar carries Morgan's mark, which
"Mend the Fence" (94203) depends on. Rung 1.

- **Title:** Princess
- **Objectives:** Kill Princess in the Maclure vineyard and bring her collar back to Ma
  Stonefield.
- **Details:** Since the Westfall families came over the ridge, every acre in Elwynn has three
  people wanting it. I can't spare a row of turnips, and the Maclures' sow has had half of them
  since spring. They call her Princess. She's the size of a calf, and she gores anyone who comes
  near. Kill her and bring me her collar, so the Maclures can't pretend they didn't know.
- **Progress:** Is the pig dead yet?
- **Completion:** That isn't a Maclure stamp on the collar. It's the mark on the letters about our
  debt, the ones the postman won't leave with me. Somebody gave that sow to the Maclures and let
  her loose on my land. I'd like to know who.

## 5. 94207 Pumpkins for Bernice

Bernice Stonefield, level 13 bounty. Gather 8 Ripe Pumpkins.

Angle: Stormwind ate Westfall's wheat, so now it eats Elwynn's, and Bernice has eleven at table
since her sister walked in from Westfall. No plot thread.

- **Title:** Pumpkins for Bernice
- **Objectives:** Gather 8 Ripe Pumpkins from the Brackwell patch, then return to Bernice
  Stonefield.
- **Details:** Stormwind ate Westfall's wheat for as long as anyone remembers. Westfall's gone
  grey, so now the city eats whatever Elwynn grows, and the buyers are at the gate before it's
  ripe. The Brackwell patch is the best we've got, and the Defias have run off the pickers. Bring
  me ripe ones, whole. I'll pay for every one.
- **Progress:** Got my pumpkins?
- **Completion:** Good and heavy. Half go on the city cart and half stay here. We've had eleven at
  table since my sister walked in from Westfall with her boys. Come back when the patch has grown
  more.

## 6. 94306 Candles for Gorn

Foreman Gorn, Fargodeep, level 15 bounty. Collect 8 Kobold Candles.

Angle: lamp oil came by ship, and the ships stopped (missing news). Half Gorn's crew are Westfall
farmhands who never held a pick. No plot thread.

- **Title:** Candles for Gorn
- **Objectives:** Bring 8 Kobold Candles to Foreman Gorn.
- **Details:** Lamp oil came in by ship. The ships stopped coming a year ago, and the court says
  it's weather. My men can't dig in the dark, and a kobold never goes anywhere without a candle on
  its head. Take them off the kobolds of Fargodeep. Eight to a lot, and I'll pay for each lot.
- **Progress:** Got my candles?
- **Completion:** Tallow and wick. It'll do. Half my crew were farmhands in Westfall till last
  spring. They'd never held a pick before. They need all the light they can get.

## 7. 94402 The Kobold King's Plea

Chieftain Grik, Jasperlode, level 17 story. Kill the Eye Below.

Angle: Grik's people want to leave, and every tunnel they know ends in grey. Reframes the Eye as
the cult's guard on the dig, per the bible. Keeps "something older than a mine." Rung 2, "It's a
cult."

- **Title:** The Kobold King's Plea
- **Objectives:** Kill the Eye Below at the bottom of Jasperlode Mine and return to Chieftain
  Grik.
- **Details:** The dark robes came with big-folk diggers and drove us to the deep tunnels. Then
  they put the Eye in the pit to guard their dig. It doesn't move. It looks, and the ones it looks
  at go quiet, and they don't come back to themselves. Kill it. Then my people walk out of Elwynn
  and trouble nobody. I swear it on the mine.
- **Progress:** Is it dead? I can still hear it hum.
- **Completion:** The hum stopped. We go tonight. Where, I don't know. Every tunnel my grandmother
  knew ends in grey now. But not here. Take this, and tell your Marshal that what dug here dug for
  something older than a mine.

## 8. 94604 Where It Pointed

Theocritus to Marshal Dughan, level 22 story. Carry the letter.

Angle: Theocritus taught Morganth for eleven years and thought him dead. Dughan's weekly reports
come back "Elwynn considered clear" (missing news, no name behind it yet). Rung 3, "The cult is
building something."

- **Title:** Where It Pointed
- **Objectives:** Take Theocritus's letter to Marshal Dughan in Goldshire.
- **Details:** Morganth was my apprentice for eleven years. I taught him to listen to ore, and then
  I mourned him. He set this rod, and the ore in it still hums toward wherever he went next. I've
  written down the bearing. Take the letter to Dughan, and tell him the tower was a signpost, not a
  prize.
- **Progress:** Have you seen the marshal yet?
- **Completion:** East, then. Toward Redridge. I send a report to Stormwind every week, and every
  week the answer comes back the same: Elwynn considered clear. Somebody isn't reading them. Thank
  you for this. Go and rest; you've earned it.

## 9. 94706 Lumber Recovery

Supervisor Raelen, Eastvale, level 22 bounty. Collect 8 Eastvale Lumber.

Angle: Stormwind wants every beam for housing, with families sleeping in the Cathedral square.
Thread: the looters' cart tracks all run to the river (tribute going upriver). Rung 3.

- **Title:** Lumber Recovery
- **Objectives:** Bring 8 Eastvale Lumber to Supervisor Raelen.
- **Details:** Stormwind's ordered every beam we can cut, for housing. There are families sleeping
  in the Cathedral square since Westfall went. Then the looters come by night and carry it off,
  and some they drop on the way. Bring me eight lengths of Eastvale Lumber and I'll pay for each
  batch.
- **Progress:** Got the lumber?
- **Completion:** Straight and sound. That's a roof for somebody. Odd thing, though. They never
  take it toward the city. Every cart track runs down to the river. Come back when they've stolen
  more.

## 10. 94801 Remy's Last Debtor

Remy to Dobbs at Jerod's Landing, level 23 story. Find Dobbs.

Angle: Dobbs's wife took their girl to Sentinel Hill the week before Westfall went. He writes the
smugglers' ledger because he has nobody left to keep anything for. Thread: every crate goes
upriver. Rung 3.

- **Title:** Remy's Last Debtor
- **Objectives:** Find Dobbs, Remy's debtor, on the dock at Jerod's Landing.
- **Details:** The man who owes me most skipped town the night the Abbey fell. Dobbs. He left his
  coat on my counter with a year of my bills in the pocket. I hear he's on the dock at Jerod's
  Landing, with men I wouldn't drink with. I don't want the money now. Dobbs drank at my counter
  for twenty years. I want to know what he's mixed up in.
- **Progress:** Did you find Dobbs?
- **Completion:** Remy sent you? Then I'm forgiven or I'm dead, and I can't tell which. My wife
  took our girl to her mother's in Sentinel Hill the week before Westfall went. I've had no letter
  since. These men pay me to write down every crate they load, and every crate goes upriver. Take
  what I know to Dughan. I've nobody left to keep it for.

## 11. 94901 A Hermit's Request (rumour 1)

Hermit Oswin, Northshire, level 5 rumour quest. Kill 8 Timber Wolves.

Angle: Oswin walked south from the Alterac foothills ahead of the plague with forty goats, and the
wolves have taken them all. No plot thread (the rumour's one thread is the refugee walk south,
which is common knowledge at any level).

- **Title:** A Hermit's Request
- **Objectives:** Kill 8 Timber Wolves for Hermit Oswin.
- **Details:** I herded goats in the Alterac foothills till the plague came. I walked south with
  forty of them, and I kept every one alive the whole way. The wolves here have done what the
  Scourge couldn't. I can't hold a blade like you can. Thin them for me.
- **Progress:** Are the wolves still about?
- **Completion:** The hills will be quieter tonight. I've nothing left to guard, but I'll sleep
  better. Thank you, stranger.

The three notes that lead to him (980030-980032), two pages each:

- **The river bank below the Abbey:** "Forty goats I walked down from Alterac. The wolves have
  every one." / "I'm no fighter. If you can hold a blade, find me on the river bank below the
  Abbey."
- **The wolf glade east of the Abbey:** "They don't wait for dark any more. They took the last
  kid at noon." / "Thin them for me. I'll be in the wolf glade east of the Abbey."
- **The hillside north of the Abbey:** "I counted forty at the spring thaw. I count none now." /
  "Find me on the hillside north of the Abbey. Bring a blade."
