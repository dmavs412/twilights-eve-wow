# Sample rewrites for review

Eleven existing quests and one rumour's notes, rewritten to fit `docs/lore-bible.md`. Nothing in
the game changes yet. Mark each one keep, change or drop, and say why. The rest of the quests will
follow the voice of the ones you keep.

Each sample keeps its quest's mobs, counts, items and chain. "Angle" names the person's story and
the one plot thread it carries, with that thread's rung on the reveal ladder.

## 1. 94005 Wolves at the Fold

Eagan Peltskinner, Northshire, level 3 bounty. Kill 10 Young or Timber Wolves.

Angle: the wolves are thin strangers driven in from the Westfall side, where Eagan's brother once
farmed. There is no plot thread; the shrinking world is the whole story.

- **Title:** Wolves at the Fold
- **Objectives:** Kill 10 Young Wolves or Timber Wolves in Northshire Valley, then return to Eagan
  Peltskinner.
- **Details:** There are more wolves every season, and they aren't ours. They come in thin and
  hungry from the west, out of the hills above Westfall, where my brother kept sheep before the
  Hush took it. Whatever is over there, they won't stay near it, so they come here, to my fold.
  I'll pay for every pack you thin, young or timber. It's all the same to me.
- **Progress:** Are they still out there? I haven't slept through a night in weeks.
- **Completion:** That will buy the fold a few quiet nights. More will come. They have nowhere
  else to go, the same as the rest of us. My coin will be here when they do.

## 2. 94006 Pelts for Peltskinner

Eagan Peltskinner, level 4 bounty. Collect 8 Northshire Wolf Pelts.

Angle: winter furs used to come down from Dun Morogh, and that road now ends in grey. There is no
plot thread.

- **Title:** Pelts for Peltskinner
- **Objectives:** Bring 8 Northshire Wolf Pelts to Eagan Peltskinner.
- **Details:** The quartermaster in Goldshire buys every pelt I send him, and his price climbs
  every month. Our furs used to come down from Dun Morogh, but that road ends in grey now, and no
  one who has walked it has come back. Either Elwynn's wolves keep the Watch warm this winter, or
  nothing does. Bring me their pelts, whole if you can manage it.
- **Progress:** Any pelts for me?
- **Completion:** Good, thick hides. That's a few more men warm at the gate this winter. Here is
  your share.

## 3. 94101 McBride's Report

Marshal McBride to Marshal Dughan, level 10 story. Carry the sealed report.

Angle: McBride has sent riders to Stormwind and heard nothing back (missing news). Dughan believes
in Defias, not cults. Rung 1, "It's a Defias matter."

- **Title:** McBride's Report
- **Objectives:** Carry Marshal McBride's sealed report to Marshal Dughan in Goldshire.
- **Details:** It's all here: the idol in the undercroft, the robed men, and the Abbot ringing the
  bell to let them in. I've sent three riders to Stormwind since the Abbey fell, and not one has
  returned with an answer. Dughan is closer, and he can't claim he never received it. Put this in
  his hands and no one else's.
- **Progress:** Have you got the report for Marshal Dughan?
- **Completion:** McBride's seal. A cult, he writes, with the Abbot ringing the bell for them.
  Brother Sammuel heard confessions in that Abbey for thirty years. I've had Defias in my fields
  since spring, and the Defias I understand. I'll read every word, but don't ask me to believe all
  of it.

## 4. 94202 Princess

Ma Stonefield, level 12 story. Kill Princess and bring back her collar.

Angle: land has been scarce since the Westfall families crossed the ridge, and the old feud with
the Maclures has teeth now. The existing thread stays: the collar carries Morgan's mark, which
"Mend the Fence" (94203) depends on. Rung 1.

- **Title:** Princess
- **Objectives:** Kill Princess in the Maclure vineyard and bring her collar back to Ma
  Stonefield.
- **Details:** Since the Westfall families came over the ridge, every acre in Elwynn has three
  people wanting it. I can't spare a single row of turnips, and the Maclures' sow has rooted up
  half of them since spring. They call her Princess. She's the size of a calf, and she gores
  anyone who comes near her. Kill her and bring me her collar, so the Maclures can't pretend they
  didn't know.
- **Progress:** Is that pig dead yet?
- **Completion:** That's no Maclure stamp on this collar. It's the same mark that's on the letters
  about our debt, the ones the postman won't leave with me. Someone gave that sow to the Maclures
  and turned her loose on my land, and I'd like to know who.

## 5. 94207 Pumpkins for Bernice

Bernice Stonefield, level 13 bounty. Gather 8 Ripe Pumpkins.

Angle: Stormwind used to eat Westfall's wheat, so now it eats Elwynn's harvest, and Bernice has
had eleven at her table since her sister walked in from Westfall. There is no plot thread.

- **Title:** Pumpkins for Bernice
- **Objectives:** Gather 8 Ripe Pumpkins from the Brackwell patch, then return to Bernice
  Stonefield.
- **Details:** For as long as anyone can remember, Stormwind ate Westfall's wheat. Now that
  Westfall's gone grey, the city eats whatever Elwynn grows, and the buyers are at the gate before
  anything is ripe. The Brackwell patch is the best we have left, but the Defias have run off the
  pickers. Bring me ripe pumpkins, whole ones, and I'll pay for every one.
- **Progress:** Have you got my pumpkins?
- **Completion:** Good and heavy. Half go on the city cart, and half stay here. We've had eleven
  at the table since my sister walked in from Westfall with her boys. Come back when the patch has
  grown more.

## 6. 94306 Candles for Gorn

Foreman Gorn, Fargodeep, level 15 bounty. Collect 8 Kobold Candles.

Angle: lamp oil came by ship, and the ships have stopped (missing news). Half of Gorn's crew are
Westfall farmhands who had never held a pick. There is no plot thread.

- **Title:** Candles for Gorn
- **Objectives:** Bring 8 Kobold Candles to Foreman Gorn.
- **Details:** Our lamp oil came in by ship. The ships stopped coming a year ago, and the court
  says it's the weather. My men can't dig in the dark, and a kobold never goes anywhere without a
  candle on its head. Take them from the kobolds in Fargodeep and bring me eight at a time. I'll
  pay for every lot.
- **Progress:** Have you got my candles?
- **Completion:** Tallow and wick. They'll do. Half my crew were farmhands in Westfall until last
  spring, and not one of them had held a pick before. They need all the light they can get.

## 7. 94402 The Kobold King's Plea

Chieftain Grik, Jasperlode, level 17 story. Kill the Eye Below.

Angle: Grik's people want to leave, and every tunnel they know ends in grey. The Eye becomes the
cult's guard on the dig, as the bible says. Grik's closing line stays word for word. Rung 2, "It's
a cult."

- **Title:** The Kobold King's Plea
- **Objectives:** Kill the Eye Below at the bottom of Jasperlode Mine and return to Chieftain
  Grik.
- **Details:** The dark robes came down with big-folk diggers and drove us into the deep tunnels.
  Then they set the Eye in the pit to guard their dig. It does not move. It watches, and the ones
  it watches go quiet and never come back to themselves. Kill it, and my people will walk out of
  Elwynn and trouble no one. I swear it on the mine.
- **Progress:** Is it dead? I can still hear it humming.
- **Completion:** The humming has stopped. We leave tonight. Where we will go, I don't know. Every
  tunnel my grandmother knew ends in grey now. But we will not stay here. Take this, and tell your
  Marshal that what dug here dug for something older than a mine.

## 8. 94604 Where It Pointed

Theocritus to Marshal Dughan, level 22 story. Carry the letter.

Angle: Theocritus taught Morganth for eleven years and believed him dead. Dughan's weekly reports
come back marked "Elwynn considered clear" (missing news, with no name behind it yet). Rung 3,
"The cult is building something."

- **Title:** Where It Pointed
- **Objectives:** Take Theocritus's letter to Marshal Dughan in Goldshire.
- **Details:** Morganth was my apprentice for eleven years. I taught him to listen to ore, and when
  he vanished, I mourned him. He set this rod, and the ore in it still hums toward wherever he went
  next. I've written down the bearing. Take this letter to Dughan, and tell him the tower was a
  signpost, not a prize.
- **Progress:** Have you seen the marshal yet?
- **Completion:** East, then, toward Redridge. I send a report to Stormwind every week, and every
  week the same answer comes back. Elwynn considered clear. Someone isn't reading them. Thank you
  for bringing this. Go and rest; you've earned it.

## 9. 94706 Lumber Recovery

Supervisor Raelen, Eastvale, level 22 bounty. Collect 8 Eastvale Lumber.

Angle: Stormwind wants every beam for housing, with families sleeping in the Cathedral square. The
thread is that the looters' cart tracks all run to the river (tribute going upriver). Rung 3.

- **Title:** Lumber Recovery
- **Objectives:** Bring 8 Eastvale Lumber to Supervisor Raelen.
- **Details:** Stormwind has ordered every beam we can cut, to house the families who've been
  sleeping in the Cathedral square since Westfall went. Then looters come by night and carry it
  off, dropping some along the way. Bring me eight lengths of Eastvale Lumber, and I'll pay for
  each batch.
- **Progress:** Have you got the lumber?
- **Completion:** Straight and sound. That's a roof for somebody. Odd thing, though. They never
  haul it toward the city. Every cart track runs down to the river. Come back when they've stolen
  more.

## 10. 94801 Remy's Last Debtor

Remy to Dobbs at Jerod's Landing, level 23 story. Find Dobbs.

Angle: Dobbs's wife took their daughter to Sentinel Hill the week before Westfall went. He keeps
the smugglers' ledger because he has no one left to keep anything for. The thread is that every
crate goes upriver. Rung 3.

- **Title:** Remy's Last Debtor
- **Objectives:** Find Dobbs, Remy's debtor, on the dock at Jerod's Landing.
- **Details:** The man who owes me the most skipped town the night the Abbey fell. Dobbs is his
  name. He left his coat on my counter with a year of my bills in the pocket. I hear he's on the
  dock at Jerod's Landing now, among men I wouldn't drink with. I don't care about the money
  anymore. Dobbs drank at my counter for twenty years, and I want to know what he's mixed up in.
- **Progress:** Did you find Dobbs?
- **Completion:** Remy sent you? Then either I'm forgiven or I'm a dead man, and I can't tell
  which. My wife took our girl to her mother's in Sentinel Hill the week before Westfall went, and
  I haven't had a letter since. These men pay me to write down every crate they load, and every
  crate goes upriver. Take what I know to Dughan. I've no one left to keep it for.

## 11. 94901 A Hermit's Request (rumour 1)

Hermit Oswin, Northshire, level 5 rumour quest. Kill 8 Timber Wolves.

Angle: Oswin walked south from the Alterac foothills ahead of the plague with forty goats, and the
wolves have taken them all. There is no plot thread; the refugee walk south is common knowledge at
any level.

- **Title:** A Hermit's Request
- **Objectives:** Kill 8 Timber Wolves for Hermit Oswin.
- **Details:** I herded goats in the Alterac foothills until the plague came. I walked south with
  forty of them and kept every one alive the whole way. The wolves here have done what the Scourge
  never could. I can't swing a blade the way you can. Thin them out for me.
- **Progress:** Are the wolves still out there?
- **Completion:** The hills will be quieter tonight. I've nothing left to guard, but I'll sleep
  better all the same. Thank you, stranger.

The three notes that lead to him (980030-980032), two pages each:

- **The river bank below the Abbey:** "I walked forty goats down from Alterac, and the wolves have
  taken every one." / "I am no fighter. If you can hold a blade, find me on the river bank below
  the Abbey."
- **The wolf glade east of the Abbey:** "They no longer wait for dark. They took the last kid at
  noon." / "Thin them out for me. I'll be in the wolf glade east of the Abbey."
- **The hillside north of the Abbey:** "I counted forty at the spring thaw. Now I count none." /
  "Find me on the hillside north of the Abbey, and bring a blade."
