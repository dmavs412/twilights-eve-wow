# Crystal Lake, Fargodeep and Jasperlode (13-18)

New text for every quest at Crystal Lake, Fargodeep and Jasperlode, and Lee Brown's angler quests.
Quests marked "approved" are the samples, unchanged. Every quest keeps its mobs, counts, items and
chain. Titles are unchanged.

Reveal ladder: rung 2, "It's a cult." Dughan accepts it at 94403.

## Crystal Lake and Fargodeep

### 94301 Narg's Orders

Marshal Dughan, level 14 story. Kill Narg and bring his journal.

Angle: the kobolds came to Fargodeep to hide, the same as half the people in Goldshire.

- **Objectives:** Kill Narg the Taskmaster at the bottom of Fargodeep Mine and bring his journal
  to Marshal Dughan. (unchanged)
- **Details:** Morgan's ledger lists ore from Fargodeep, shipped by the cartload. The kobolds there
  answer to a taskmaster called Narg, who keeps his orders in a journal and his pick at the bottom
  of the mine. Kill him and bring me the journal. I want to know who told a kobold to dig for the
  Defias.
- **Progress:** Do you have Narg's journal yet?
- **Completion:** Narg didn't write this for the Defias. He writes about the kobolds who fled
  Jasperlode, driven out by the ones who dig down instead of across. They didn't come to Fargodeep
  to work. They came to hide, the same as half the people in my town. Whatever is digging in
  Jasperlode is no kobold.

### 94302 The Dead Courier

Started by the Courier's Satchel, taken to Marshal Dughan, level 14 story.

Angle: the courier is Aldo Finch, whose mother sells eggs by the well. The bible expands the
dispatch: it was falsified, and the courier was killed for seeing the original. At level 14 only
the wrongness shows: wounds that aren't a wolf's, and a dispatch that says "Elwynn considered
clear." Nothing points at Prestor's office (rung 6).

- **Objectives:** Bring the Courier's Satchel to Marshal Dughan in Goldshire. (unchanged)
- **Details:** A Stormwind courier lies dead beside his satchel. His wounds are not a wolf's. The
  satchel bears the seal of the regent's office, and it is addressed to the Marshal at Goldshire.
  Take it to him.
- **Progress:** Is that the courier's satchel?
- **Completion:** Aldo Finch. His mother sells eggs by the well. I'll tell her myself. The dispatch
  reads: disturbances in Redridge under review, Elwynn considered clear, no further patrols
  required. Considered clear! I've sent that office a report every week since the Abbey fell.
  Either nobody there reads them, or they read them and don't believe a word. Aldo died a day's
  walk from home to carry this.

### 94303 The Keystone

Foreman Gorn, level 15 story. Kill Gllrg and bring the keystone.

Angle: Gorn means to bring every man on his crew home every night.

- **Objectives:** Kill Gllrg the Tidecaller at Crystal Lake and bring the Rune-marked Keystone to
  Foreman Gorn. (unchanged)
- **Details:** Narg's journal tells me what we feared. The inner door of Jasperlode is sealed, and
  the stone that opens it was kept at the old mine entrance. The murlocs at Crystal Lake carried
  it off, and Gllrg the Tidecaller wears it around his neck. Kill him and bring me the keystone.
  The door opens for no one without it.
- **Progress:** The keystone, if you please.
- **Completion:** Rune-marked, and the runes still glow. That's the key to the sealed door in
  Jasperlode. Keep it. Whatever is behind that door is no business for a foreman. I have a crew to
  bring home every night, and I mean to bring home all of them.

### 94305 Kobolds in Fargodeep

Foreman Gorn, level 15 bounty. Kill 10 Kobold Tunnelers or Kobold Geomancers.

Angle: with the Ironforge tram stopped, Fargodeep is all the iron the city has.

- **Objectives:** Kill 10 Kobold Tunnelers or Kobold Geomancers, then return to Foreman Gorn.
  (unchanged)
- **Details:** The city needs iron, and with the Ironforge tram stopped, Fargodeep is what it has.
  The tunnelers dig wherever I tell them not to, and the geomancers chant over the holes. I can't
  send my own men in. Put down ten of either, and I'll pay a bounty each time you come back.
- **Progress:** Are the tunnels any quieter?
- **Completion:** Ten fewer holes to mind. They'll dig more, and I'll pay for those too.

### 94306 Candles for Gorn (approved)

Foreman Gorn, level 15 bounty.

- **Objectives:** Bring 8 Kobold Candles to Foreman Gorn.
- **Details:** Our lamp oil came in by ship. The ships stopped coming a year ago, and the court
  says it's the weather. My men can't dig in the dark, and a kobold never goes anywhere without a
  candle on its head. Take them from the kobolds in Fargodeep and bring me eight at a time. I'll
  pay for every lot.
- **Progress:** Have you got my candles?
- **Completion:** Tallow and wick. They'll do. Half my crew were farmhands in Westfall until last
  spring, and not one of them had held a pick before. They need all the light they can get.

### 94307 Murlocs on the Shore

Fisherman Lee Brown, level 15 bounty. Kill 10 Murloc Foragers or Murloc Lurkers.

Angle: these are coast murlocs, come up the river since the sea went grey. Lee knows the lake ones.

- **Objectives:** Kill 10 Murloc Foragers or Murloc Lurkers, then return to Fisherman Lee Brown.
  (unchanged)
- **Details:** These aren't lake murlocs. I've fished Crystal Lake for thirty years, and I know the
  lake ones. These came up the river from the coast after the sea went grey, and there are more
  every month. They scare the fish off, and my nets come up empty. Kill ten of the foragers or the
  lurkers, and I'll pay you, as often as you like.
- **Progress:** Are there any murlocs left on my shore?
- **Completion:** Ten fewer on the shore. I'd almost feel sorry for them, if they'd leave my nets
  alone. Here's your coin.

### 94308 Fins for Lee Brown

Fisherman Lee Brown, level 16 bounty. Collect 8 Murloc Fins.

Angle: Farley needs every pot of stew he can get with his floor full of sleepers.

- **Objectives:** Bring 8 Murloc Fins to Fisherman Lee Brown. (unchanged)
- **Details:** A murloc fin makes good bait and better stew, and Farley needs every pot of stew he
  can get with his floor full of sleepers. Bring me eight from the lake's murlocs, and I'll pay a
  fair price whenever you have more.
- **Progress:** Have you got my fins?
- **Completion:** Fresh fins, and a good lot. The stew will stretch further tonight. Come back for
  more.

## Lee Brown's angler quests

### 96101 Smallfry for the Lion's Pride

Level 3 repeatable. Bring 10 Raw Brilliant Smallfish.

- **Objectives:** Bring 10 Raw Brilliant Smallfish to Fisherman Lee Brown. (unchanged)
- **Details:** The Lion's Pride feeds half of Goldshire, and I feed the Lion's Pride. Smallfish go
  into the pot whole, and I never have enough of them. Bring me ten from the lake, and I'll pay you
  for the lot, as often as you can fill a bucket.
- **Progress:** Is that a bucket of smallfish, or are you just glad to see me? (unchanged)
- **Completion:** Ten fat smallfish, and still wet. Farley's cook will be pleased. Here's your
  coin. Come back when you have more.

### 96102 Snapper Supper

Level 6 repeatable. Bring 8 Raw Longjaw Mud Snapper.

- **Objectives:** Bring 8 Raw Longjaw Mud Snapper to Fisherman Lee Brown. (unchanged)
- **Details:** A longjaw snapper makes a proper supper, and the inn's regulars know it. They're
  hard work to land, but the deep water of the lake gives them up. Bring me eight, and I'll pay a
  silver, and the same again whenever you have another eight.
- **Progress:** Have you got my snappers? The regulars are getting restless.
- **Completion:** Eight good snappers. They'll be on every plate in the Pride tonight. A silver
  for you, and my thanks. (unchanged)

### 96103 A Trophy for the Wall

Level 9 repeatable. Bring a 10 Pound Mud Snapper.

Angle: the fish over Lee's bar is a sea bass he took off the Stormwind breakwater at twenty.
Nobody fishes past the breakwater now.

- **Objectives:** Bring a 10 Pound Mud Snapper to Fisherman Lee Brown. (unchanged)
- **Details:** The fish over my bar is a sea bass I took off the Stormwind breakwater when I was
  twenty. Nobody fishes past the breakwater now, so there will never be another one like it. It's
  time it came down, before I start talking to it. A ten pound mud snapper would do for the wall.
  They're rare, and they only take a patient line. Bring me one, and I'll pay three silver.
- **Progress:** Any sign of that trophy snapper?
- **Completion:** Look at the size of it! That's the one for the wall. I'll hang the old bass in
  the back, where I can still see it. Three silver, and you've earned every copper.

## Jasperlode Mine

### 94401 Into Jasperlode

Marshal Dughan to Chieftain Grik, level 15 story. Open the sealed door.

Angle: Dughan doesn't yet say "cult" (he does at 94403), so the diggers below are "whoever is doing
the digging."

- **Objectives:** Open the sealed door in Jasperlode Mine with the keystone and find Chieftain
  Grik's camp inside. (unchanged)
- **Details:** You have the keystone, and the sealed door in Jasperlode is the door it fits. The
  mine is split in two. The kobolds who wouldn't leave are above, and whoever is doing the digging
  is below. Open the door and find Chieftain Grik. If anyone in there will talk, he will.
- **Progress:** You haven't opened the door yet.
- **Completion:** You are the first of the big folk to come through that door. Sit, but keep your
  voice down. The thing below hears loud voices. (unchanged)

### 94402 The Kobold King's Plea (approved)

Chieftain Grik, level 17 story.

- **Objectives:** Kill the Eye Below at the bottom of Jasperlode Mine and return to Chieftain
  Grik.
- **Details:** The dark robes came down with big-folk diggers and drove us into the deep tunnels.
  Then they set the Eye in the pit to guard their dig. It does not move. It watches, and the ones
  it watches go quiet and never come back to themselves. Kill it, and my people will walk out of
  Elwynn and trouble no one. I swear it on the mine.
- **Progress:** Is it dead? I can still hear it humming.
- **Completion:** The humming has stopped. We leave tonight. Where we will go, I don't know. Every
  tunnel my grandmother knew ends in grey now. But we will not stay here. Take this, and tell your
  Marshal that what dug here dug for something older than a mine.

### 94403 What Narg Feared

Marshal Dughan, level 18 story. Bring the Dig Orders from the Eye Below.

Angle: the bible makes the Eye a guard the cult grew, not a commander, so the orders were left for
the diggers it guarded. Dughan's apology is rung 2.

- **Objectives:** Bring the Dig Orders from the Eye Below to Marshal Dughan. (unchanged)
- **Details:** Narg wrote that the ones who dig down took the mine. A thing like that Eye doesn't
  dig, and it doesn't give orders. Someone put it in that pit to guard the work, and whoever did
  that left orders for the diggers. Search what's left of it, and bring me what you find.
- **Progress:** Did you find any orders?
- **Completion:** The same mark as the idol from Echo Ridge, in the same hand. I called this a
  Defias matter for far too long. It was never the Defias. Forgive an old soldier, and let's see
  what else this hand has signed.

### 94404 Resonant Ore

Smith Argus, level 17 repeatable bounty. Collect 10 Resonant Ore.

Angle: the bible's ringing-ore motif. Argus's apprentice handled one piece and can't stand a quiet
room since. Argus will buy it but never forge with it.

- **Objectives:** Bring 10 Resonant Ore to Smith Argus in Goldshire. (unchanged)
- **Details:** Strangers have bought my ore in bulk, and now I hear the kobolds in Jasperlode are
  digging up something that rings when you strike it. Resonant ore, they call it. My apprentice
  handled one piece last week, and now he can't stand a quiet room. He hums to himself all day. I
  want to know why. Bring me ten pieces, and again whenever you find more.
- **Progress:** Does it still ring?
- **Completion:** It rings, all right. Hold it up to your ear. I don't like what I hear, but I'll
  pay for more of it. I won't forge with it. Not ever.
