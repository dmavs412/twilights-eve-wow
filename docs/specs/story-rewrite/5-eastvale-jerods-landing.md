# Eastvale, Ridgepoint, Mirror Lake and Jerod's Landing (20-26)

New text for every quest in Eastvale, Ridgepoint, Mirror Lake and Jerod's Landing. Quests marked
"approved" are the samples, unchanged. Every quest keeps its mobs, counts, items and chain. Titles
are unchanged.

Reveal ladder: rung 3, "The cult is building something." The tribute goes upriver.

## Eastvale and Ridgepoint

### 94701 Timber for the Pit

Supervisor Raelen, level 21 story. Kill Defias Overseer Hadrik and bring his manifest.

Angle: half the camp's timber goes out on Defias carts, and the city gets the rest. Fits 94706,
where the city orders every beam.

- **Objectives:** Kill Defias Overseer Hadrik at the lumber mill and bring his manifest back to
  Supervisor Raelen. (unchanged)
- **Details:** Half of what this camp cuts goes out on a Defias cart, and the city gets the rest.
  I've stopped asking why. The loads are cut to scaffold lengths, which are no use to a farmer or
  a shipwright, not that anyone is building ships. Their overseer, Hadrik, works the east sawmill
  and keeps a manifest of every load. Kill him and bring me that paper.
- **Progress:** Has Hadrik's manifest come to hand?
- **Completion:** Scaffold lengths, every one, addressed to the Landing, for the pit. A pit with
  scaffolding is a dig, and a dig at Jerod's Landing is no Defias business. Here's your coin. Keep
  your eyes on the ridge.

### 94702 Eyes on the Ridge

Supervisor Raelen, level 22 story. Reach the three overlooks unseen.

Angle: Raelen's father stood watch at Ridgepoint.

- **Objectives:** Reach the three overlooks above Ridgepoint Tower without being seen, then return
  to Supervisor Raelen with Dughan's letter. (unchanged)
- **Details:** Dughan's letter says the signpost points at Ridgepoint Tower, the old Alliance
  watch. My father stood watch there when I was young. I need to know what flies from it now. Go
  up to the north, east and south overlooks and look. Don't fight anything on the way. If they see
  you, the cult will know we know, and the whole ridge will be shut to us.
- **Progress:** What do you see on the tower?
- **Completion:** Black-sun banners on the walls, in daylight, on an Alliance watchtower. Three or
  four of them, by your count, and nobody on the road thought to look up. My father would have
  wept. Thank you for not being seen.

### 94703 Ridgepoint Falls

Supervisor Raelen, level 23 story. Kill Speaker Vael and bring his notes.

Angle: some of Vael's acolytes cut timber for Raelen last year.

- **Objectives:** Kill Speaker Vael on the top floor of Ridgepoint Tower and bring his notes to
  Supervisor Raelen. (unchanged)
- **Details:** Now we know who holds the tower. Speaker Vael speaks for the cult on this ridge, and
  he sits on the top floor behind his acolytes. Some of those acolytes cut timber for me last year.
  Go in, fight your way up, and put him down. Bring me whatever he carries.
- **Progress:** Is Vael dead?
- **Completion:** Vael's notes. The hand is shaky, as if he wrote while he fell: the gnoll was
  promised power; he digs for us. The gnoll at the pit is no ally and no thief. He's a tool they
  promised the world to. That's worth more than the tower. Here's your reward.

### 94704 Toren's Trail

Started by Toren's Pack, taken to Toren, level 23 story.

Angle: the bible says Morganth ordered Toren kept alive and unharmed, because Toren is the one
person he still cares about. Toren can feel that someone ordered it, and can't think who would
care.

- **Objectives:** Take Toren's Pack to Toren in Ridgepoint Tower. (unchanged)
- **Details:** A pack lies on the floor with its straps cut. Toren's name is sewn into the flap:
  Theocritus's apprentice, who waited outside the Tower of Azora. He's here somewhere on this
  floor. Find him.
- **Progress:** Is that my pack?
- **Completion:** My pack, and you aren't one of them. I went looking for Morganth, and the cult
  found me first. They didn't pay me, and they didn't hurt me. They kept me, fed and unharmed, as
  if someone had ordered it. I can't think who in this world would care. Tell Dughan I'm alive,
  and that I'll tell him the rest myself.

### 94706 Lumber Recovery (approved)

Supervisor Raelen, level 22 bounty.

- **Objectives:** Bring 8 Eastvale Lumber to Supervisor Raelen.
- **Details:** Stormwind has ordered every beam we can cut, to house the families who've been
  sleeping in the Cathedral square since Westfall went. Then looters come by night and carry it

### 94707 Bears on the Ridge

Lumberjack Tomas, level 23 bounty. Kill 10 Grizzled Bears.

Angle: Tomas's family has cut Eastvale for four generations. His grandfather cut the beams for the
Cathedral.

- **Objectives:** Kill 10 Grizzled Bears, then return to Lumberjack Tomas. (unchanged)
- **Details:** My family has cut Eastvale for four generations. My grandfather cut the beams for

### 94709 Acolytes at Ridgepoint

Supervisor Raelen, level 24 bounty. Kill 10 Twilight Acolytes.

Angle: Corin worked Raelen's saw for eight years. His wife walked into the grey last winter, and
the cult told him the Hush was a god and that she was with it.

- **Objectives:** Kill 10 Twilight Acolytes at Ridgepoint Tower, then return to Supervisor Raelen.
  (unchanged)
- **Details:** Corin worked my saw for eight years. His wife walked into the grey last winter, and
  in the spring he walked up that ridge in a robe. They told him the Hush was a god, and that she

  rather you didn't tell me. Take your coin.

## Mirror Lake and Jerod's Landing

### 94801 Remy's Last Debtor (approved)

Remy to Dobbs, level 23 story.

- **Objectives:** Find Dobbs, Remy's debtor, on the dock at Jerod's Landing.
- **Details:** The man who owes me the most skipped town the night the Abbey fell. Dobbs is his
  name. He left his coat on my counter with a year of my bills in the pocket. I hear he's on the
  dock at Jerod's Landing now, among men I wouldn't drink with. I don't care about the money
  anymore. Dobbs drank at my counter for twenty years, and I want to know what he's mixed up in.
- **Progress:** Did you find Dobbs?
- **Completion:** Remy sent you? Then either I'm forgiven or I'm a dead man, and I can't tell
  which. My wife took our girl to her mother's in Sentinel Hill the week before Westfall went, and
  I haven't had a letter since. These men pay me to write down every crate they load, and every
  crate goes upriver. Take what I know to Dughan. I've no one left to keep it for.

### 94802 The Smugglers' Ledger

Marshal Dughan, level 24 story. Destroy 6 Twilight Supply Caches.

Angle: each cache is someone's work, taken from someone who needed it.

- **Objectives:** Destroy 6 Twilight Supply Caches around the dock, the farmhouse and the silo at
  Jerod's Landing, then return to Marshal Dughan. (unchanged)
- **Details:** Dobbs's ledger and Vael's notes tell the same story from two ends. Jerod's Landing
  is where the cult stores what it takes out of Elwynn before it goes upriver. Ore from Fargodeep,
  timber from Eastvale, pumpkins from the Farmlands and relics from the Abbey. Each cache is
  someone's work, taken from someone who needed it. Go to the Landing and break open six of them,
  and be quick about it. The smugglers rebuild.
- **Progress:** Are the caches broken?
- **Completion:** Ore, timber, pumpkins and relics, every one stolen from a farm or a family I know
  by name. Six caches fewer, and the dock goes quiet for a night. Thank you.

### 94803 The Pit

Sergeant Keane, level 25 story. Kill Acolyte Marren and bring his orders.

Angle: Keane asked Stormwind for twenty men and got a letter saying the matter was in hand
(missing news).

- **Objectives:** Kill Acolyte Marren at the dig in the barn at Jerod's Landing and bring his
  orders to Sergeant Keane. (unchanged)
- **Details:** I hold this post at the lake, and I watch the carts go by in the dark. Every one of
  them heads upriver. The Landing is theirs, and the barn is where they dig. Acolyte Marren runs
  the digging, with three more beside him, and I don't have the men to take the barn. I asked
  Stormwind for twenty. They sent a letter saying the matter was in hand. Kill Marren and bring me
  whatever orders he carries.
- **Progress:** Is Marren dead?
- **Completion:** Marren's orders. They read: "Hogger's camp is the staging ground. Move at the
  new moon." That's the gnolls' camp at the Forest's Edge, and the new moon is close. The dig in
  the barn was their second, and it won't be their last. Here's your reward.
