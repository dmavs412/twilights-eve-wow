# Goldshire and the Farmlands (10-14)

New text for every quest in Goldshire and the Farmlands. Quests marked "approved" are the samples,
unchanged. Every quest keeps its mobs, counts, items and chain. Titles are unchanged.

Reveal ladder: rung 1, "It's a Defias matter." The strange coin appears, unnamed. Nobody says
"Twilight coin"; that's the bible's name, not the farmers'.

## Goldshire

### 94101 McBride's Report (approved)

Marshal McBride to Marshal Dughan, level 10 story.

- **Objectives:** Carry Marshal McBride's sealed report to Marshal Dughan in Goldshire.
- **Details:** It's all here: the idol in the undercroft, the robed men, and the Abbot ringing the
  bell to let them in. I've sent three riders to Stormwind since the Abbey fell, and not one has
  returned with an answer. Dughan is closer, and he can't claim he never received it. Put this in
  his hands and no one else's.
- **Progress:** Have you got the report for Marshal Dughan?
- **Completion:** McBride's seal. A cult, he writes, with the Abbot ringing the bell for them.
  Brother Sammuel heard confessions in that Abbey for thirty years. I've had Defias in my fields
  since spring, and the Defias I understand. I'll read every word, but don't ask me to believe all
  of it.

### 94102 The Marshal's Doubt

Marshal Dughan, level 11 story. Ask Farley, Argus and Remy about the mark.

Angle: each witness has their own trouble. Farley feeds families on his common-room floor. Argus
has had no iron since the Ironforge tram stopped. The clues are unchanged.

- **Objectives:** Ask Innkeeper Farley, Smith Argus and Remy "Two Times" whether they have seen
  the hammer-and-black-sun mark, then return to Marshal Dughan. (unchanged)
- **Details:** One idol and one frightened marshal's word. That isn't enough to rouse the
  Stormwind garrison, and it isn't enough for me. Ask around Goldshire. Farley hears everything
  that passes through the inn, Argus knows who buys his ore, and Remy knows who owes what. If that
  mark has been seen here, one of them has seen it.
- **Progress:** Well? Has anyone in town seen that mark?
- **Completion:** A crate off a wrecked cart. Strangers buying ore by the cartload. A debtor who
  ran the very night the Abbey fell. That's three things I can't wave away. It's still a Defias
  matter, mind. But it's a matter.
- **Farley's reply (npc 295):** That mark? Aye, I've seen it. It was on a crate that fell
  off a cart wrecked on the Stormwind road a week back. Nobody came for the crate, and nobody will
  say what was in it. I'd have gone to look myself, but I have families sleeping on my common-room
  floor, and someone has to feed them.
- **Argus's reply (npc 514):** Strangers, buying ore by the cartload and paying in coin
  I've never seen the like of. Since the tram to Ironforge stopped running, every smith in the city
  has been begging for iron, and these men want raw ore instead. They won't say what they're
  building. I don't ask, and I don't sell them my good iron.
- **Remy's reply (npc 241):** A mark like that? Can't say I have. But my biggest debtor
  skipped town the night the Abbey fell, and he left without his coat. I don't take that for a
  coincidence.

### 94103 The Wrecked Cart

Innkeeper Farley to Marshal Dughan, level 12 story. Recover the Sealed Strongbox.

Angle: Farley would go himself, but thirty people are waiting on his soup.

- **Objectives:** Recover the Sealed Strongbox from the Defias Highwayman at the wrecked cart on
  the Stormwind road, then take it to Marshal Dughan. (unchanged)
- **Details:** The crate I told the Marshal about came off a cart that wrecked north of here on the
  Stormwind road. Now a Defias highwayman has camped beside it, and he's guarding something.
  Nobody guards an empty crate. Take whatever he's sitting on to the Marshal. I'd go myself, but
  the soup won't stir itself, and there are thirty mouths waiting on it.
- **Progress:** Did you find that strongbox?
- **Completion:** Coin I've never seen before, and a letter addressed to the Collector, at
  Brackwell. The Defias don't pay in this coin. South, then. I'll keep an eye on Brackwell.

### 94105 Prowlers on the Road

Deputy Rainer, level 12 bounty. Kill 10 Prowlers or Forest Spiders.

Angle: every week more people walk out of Stormwind looking for farm work, with their children and
a bundle, and the animals take the stragglers.

- **Objectives:** Kill 10 Prowlers or Forest Spiders near Goldshire, then return to Deputy Rainer.
  (unchanged)
- **Details:** Every week more people walk out of Stormwind looking for farm work, and they walk
  the forest road with nothing but a bundle and their children. Prowlers drag off the stragglers'
  goats, and the forest spiders make the road deadly after dark. I can't put a guard on every mile
  of it. Thin them out, and I'll pay a bounty each time. Prowlers or spiders, either will do.
- **Progress:** Is the road any safer?
- **Completion:** Ten fewer teeth on the road. They'll creep back. They always do. My coin will be
  waiting when they do.

### 94106 Silk for the Smith

Smith Argus, level 12 bounty. Collect 8 Forest Spider Silk.

Angle: his bellows rope was Kul Tiran hemp, and the last ship that carried any never came back.

- **Objectives:** Bring 8 Forest Spider Silk to Smith Argus. (unchanged)
- **Details:** My bellows rope was Kul Tiran hemp, and the last ship that carried any never came
  back. Spider silk makes a better rope than hemp ever did, and the forest is full of spiders.
  Bring me silk, eight at a time, and I'll pay you fairly for the trouble. Mind the bites.
- **Progress:** Have you got that silk?
- **Completion:** Strong stuff. That will keep my forge breathing through the week. Come back when
  you have more.

### 94107 Bandits on the Stormwind Road

Deputy Rainer, level 13 bounty. Kill 10 Defias Bandits.

Angle: with few merchants left, the bandits rob families carrying everything they own. Rainer
watched a woman hand over her husband's wedding ring.

- **Objectives:** Kill 10 Defias Bandits, then return to Deputy Rainer. (unchanged)
- **Details:** The bandits on the Stormwind road used to rob merchants. There aren't many
  merchants left, so now they rob families carrying everything they own. Yesterday I watched a
  woman hand over her husband's wedding ring, and I was too far away to do a thing about it. Every
  bandit you put down is one fewer on that road. The bounty stands as long as there are bandits to
  hunt.
- **Progress:** Are there still plenty of bandits about?
- **Completion:** Ten fewer bandits. If only there weren't always ten more. Here's your share.

### 94108 Gold Dust for Remy

Remy "Two Times", level 12 bounty. Collect 8 Gold Dust.

Angle: everyone in Goldshire is selling, and nobody is buying anything but gold.

- **Objectives:** Bring 8 Gold Dust from Kobold Vermin and Kobold Tunnelers to Remy "Two Times".
  (unchanged)
- **Details:** The kobolds at the edge of Fargodeep dig up flecks of gold and don't know what to do
  with them. I do. Everyone in Goldshire is selling, and nobody is buying anything but gold. When
  the world gets smaller, gold gets heavier. Bring me the dust, eight at a time, and I'll make it
  worth your while. Twice, if you're quick about it.
- **Progress:** Have you got my dust?
- **Completion:** Pure enough. Don't ask where it goes. Come back when you have more, and I'll pay
  you again.

## The Farmlands

### 94201 The Collector's Letter

Marshal Dughan to Guard Thomas, level 12 story.

Angle: Morgan buys the debts of struggling farms, and since the crown fixed the price of grain,
every farm is struggling.

- **Objectives:** Show the Collector's letter to Guard Thomas at the Stonefield and Maclure
  crossroads. (unchanged)
- **Details:** The letter from the strongbox is addressed to the Collector, at Brackwell. I don't
  know the name, but a man who walks the farm roads all day might. Guard Thomas stands at the
  crossroads between the Stonefield and Maclure farms. Show him the letter, and hear what he says.
- **Progress:** - (unchanged)
- **Completion:** Morgan. Aye, I know the name. He's spent the past year buying the debts of every
  farm that's struggling, and since the crown fixed the price of grain, that's every farm. The
  Stonefields owe him, and so do the Maclures. Neither family knows the other owes him too. Let me
  think on that.

### 94202 Princess (approved)

Ma Stonefield, level 12 story.

- **Objectives:** Kill Princess in the Maclure vineyard and bring her collar back to Ma
  Stonefield.
- **Details:** Since the Westfall families came over the ridge, every acre in Elwynn has three
  people wanting it. I can't spare a single row of turnips, and the Maclures' sow has rooted up
  half of them since spring. They call her Princess. She's the size of a calf, and she gores
  anyone who comes near her. Kill her and bring me her collar, so the Maclures can't pretend they
  didn't know.
- **Progress:** Is that pig dead yet?
- **Completion:** That's no Maclure stamp on this collar. It's the same mark that's on the letters
  about our debt, the ones the postman won't leave with me. Someone gave that sow to the Maclures
  and turned her loose on my land, and I'd like to know who.

### 94203 Mend the Fence

Guard Thomas, level 13 story. Show the collar to both farms.

Angle: there's too little land left in the world to waste it fighting over a fence.

- **Objectives:** Show Princess's collar to Ma Stonefield and to Joshua Maclure, then return to
  Guard Thomas. (unchanged)
- **Details:** Morgan holds both families' debts, and the pig wore his mark. I think someone has
  kept these two farms at each other's throats on purpose. Take the collar to Ma Stonefield and to
  Joshua Maclure, and watch their faces when they see it. If I'm right, they'll find they have
  more in common than a fence.
- **Progress:** Did they see the collar?
- **Completion:** So the feud was bought. Each of them thought the other had done it, and now they
  know better. There's too little land left in this world to waste it fighting over a fence. I'll
  tell them to mend it together, and I'll hold Morgan to account if I can.
- **Ma Stonefield's reply (npc 244):** That's Princess's collar, with the stamp I showed
  you. I thought Joshua Maclure set his pig on me out of spite. But the stamp isn't his. Whoever
  gave him that sow wanted her loose on my land. Joshua may know more than he thinks.
- **Joshua Maclure's reply (npc 258):** That's Princess's collar. I never wanted that sow.
  A man gave her to me and said she'd make a fine pet, and then she broke out and tore up Ma
  Stonefield's fields. I paid for every row, and I never knew why she ran. Whoever stamped this
  collar owes us both an apology.

### 94204 Morgan the Collector

Marshal Dughan, level 14 story. Kill Morgan and bring his ledger.

Angle: Dughan's last line ends rung 1. "Twilight coin" becomes "that coin," since nobody in
Goldshire has a name for it.

- **Objectives:** Kill Morgan the Collector in Brackwell manor and bring his ledger to Marshal
  Dughan. (unchanged)
- **Details:** Thomas tells me the farms have stopped fighting, and that Morgan is the reason they
  started. He keeps his books in the manor at Brackwell, behind Defias guards he pays in that
  strange coin. Kill him and bring me his ledger. I want to see whose names are on his list.
- **Progress:** Is the Collector's ledger yours yet?
- **Completion:** Defias wages, paid in that coin, line after line. And here, in the margin: road
  to the Landing, keep clear. They paid the Defias to keep the farmers fighting so that nobody
  watched the road to Jerod's Landing. This is no Defias matter anymore.

### 94205 Thugs in the Pumpkin Patch

Guard Thomas, level 13 bounty. Kill 10 Defias Thugs.

Angle: old Bridenbecker has no sons left to help him. One died at the Abbey, and the other walked
west the week after and never came back.

- **Objectives:** Kill 10 Defias Thugs, then return to Guard Thomas. (unchanged)
- **Details:** The Defias have made a camp of the Brackwell pumpkin patch, and old Bridenbecker
  can't tend his own field. He has no sons left to help him. One died at the Abbey, and the other
  walked west the week after and never came back. I can't leave the crossroads. You can. Put down
  ten thugs, and I'll pay a bounty each time you come back.

### 94206 Boars in the Vineyard

Joshua Maclure, level 13 bounty. Kill 10 Stonetusk Boars.

Angle: his son joined the Watch, saying a man ought to guard something now that there's less of
it to guard.

- **Objectives:** Kill 10 Stonetusk Boars, then return to Joshua Maclure. (unchanged)
- **Details:** My boy joined the Watch last month. He says a man ought to guard something now that
  there's less of it to guard. So it's me, the vines and the boars, and the boars are winning.
  They come down every night and take more than they can eat. Kill ten Stonetusks, and I'll pay
  you for it, and again whenever you like.
- **Progress:** Have the boars been thinned?
- **Completion:** Ten fewer boars. The vines may live through the season yet. Here's your coin.
  If you see my boy in Goldshire, tell him the vines are fine.

### 94207 Pumpkins for Bernice (approved)

Bernice Stonefield, level 13 bounty.

- **Objectives:** Gather 8 Ripe Pumpkins from the Brackwell patch, then return to Bernice
  Stonefield.
- **Details:** For as long as anyone can remember, Stormwind ate Westfall's wheat. Now that
  Westfall's gone grey, the city eats whatever Elwynn grows, and the buyers are at the gate before
  anything is ripe. The Brackwell patch is the best we have left, but the Defias have run off the
  pickers. Bring me ripe pumpkins, whole ones, and I'll pay for every one.
- **Progress:** Have you got my pumpkins?
- **Completion:** Good and heavy. Half go on the city cart, and half stay here. We've had eleven
  at the table since my sister walked in from Westfall with her boys. Come back when the patch has
  grown more.

### 94208 Boar Ribs for the Inn

Innkeeper Farley, level 14 bounty. Collect 8 Boar Ribs.

Angle: every bed in the Lion's Pride is taken, and half the floor. Families from the west, a pair
of sailors with no ship, an old woman from Lordaeron who sings in her sleep.

- **Objectives:** Bring 8 Boar Ribs to Innkeeper Farley. (unchanged)
- **Details:** Every bed in the Lion's Pride is taken, and half the common-room floor besides.
  Families from the west, a pair of sailors with no ship, an old woman from Lordaeron who sings in
  her sleep. They all have to eat, and my cellar is empty. The Stonetusk boars in the farmlands

- **Completion:** Good, meaty ribs. Nobody under my roof goes to bed hungry tonight. Come back with
