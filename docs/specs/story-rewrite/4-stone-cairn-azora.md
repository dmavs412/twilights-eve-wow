# Stone Cairn Lake and the Tower of Azora (16-22)

New text for every quest at Heroes' Vigil, Stone Cairn Lake and the Tower of Azora. Quests marked
"approved" are the samples, unchanged. Every quest keeps its mobs, counts, items and chain. Titles
are unchanged.

Reveal ladder: rungs 2 and 3. The cult marks a place before it takes it, and the tribute moves by
water.

## Heroes' Vigil and Stone Cairn Lake

### 94501 The Vigil Defaced

Grave-tender Ilsa, level 16 story. Clean 5 cairns, then kill 6 Twilight Daubers.

Angle: half the new stones at the Vigil have nobody under them. People bring a stone for a son who
walked into the grey, or a sister on a ship that never came home.

- **Objectives:** Clean 5 defaced cairns at Heroes' Vigil, then kill the 6 Twilight Daubers who
  return at dusk, and report to Grave-tender Ilsa. (unchanged)
- **Details:** Half the new stones at the Vigil have nobody under them. People bring a stone for a
  son who walked into the grey, or a sister on a ship that never came home, and I tend them like
  the rest. Now someone has painted five of the cairns, and the paint won't wash off. It's the same
  mark that was on the idol from Echo Ridge. Scrub the stones while the light lasts. Whoever
  painted them comes back at dusk to admire the work. They're fools who run when struck. Put them
  down.
- **Progress:** Are the stones clean? Is it done?
- **Completion:** The stones are clean, and the painters are down. Low cultists, and they ran at
  the first blow. But they came to my graves with a brush, and that's how the cult works. They
  mark a place before they take it.

### 94502 Rites at the Stone

Grave-tender Ilsa, level 18 story. Hold the cairn against three waves and kill Ritualist Serane.

Angle: the old text said "the dead at the Vigil are quiet no longer," which points at rung 5 (the
dead aren't silenced, Duskwood). The marks are a survey instead.

- **Objectives:** Hold Heroes' Vigil's cairn against the cultists who come up from the shore, kill
  Ritualist Serane, and return to Grave-tender Ilsa. (unchanged)
- **Details:** The marks on the cairns weren't vandalism. They were a survey, and the cult is
  coming tonight to claim what it marked. Stand at the stone. They'll come up from the shore in
  three waves, and the last has a ritualist at its head. Kill her before she finishes.
- **Progress:** Is the ritualist dead? Is the stone still ours?
- **Completion:** Serane's orders were in her hand when she fell: the tower is ready, and the
  tribute moves by water. The tower can only be Azora, and the water is the lake. They didn't come
  for the Vigil's dead. They were using the Vigil to hide a boat.

### 94503 An Echo in the Ore

Started by the Resonant Shard, taken to Toren, level 19 story.

Angle: Toren has slept on the tower steps for a month, waiting for the doors to open. He still
believes Theocritus set the rod; 94602 corrects him.

- **Objectives:** Bring the Resonant Shard to Toren, Theocritus's apprentice, outside the Tower of
  Azora. (unchanged)
- **Details:** The shard hums against your palm, a note too low to hear and too steady to be a
  trick of the ear. The ore in Jasperlode rang like this. Someone at the Tower of Azora will know
  what it is. Theocritus's apprentice waits outside the door.
- **Progress:** Is that a shard of resonant ore? Let me see it.
- **Completion:** I know this sound. The master set a rod of this ore in the tower's heart, and it
  hummed just like this. Then the doors sealed with him inside, and I've been sleeping on these
  steps for a month, waiting for them to open. If the cult has the ore too, then the tower isn't
  the master's anymore.

### 94505 Murlocs at the Cairn

Guard Pell, level 18 bounty. Kill 10 Murloc Nestguards or Murloc Tidehunters.

Angle: there used to be six guards at this post. The other five were called to the city in
spring, and nobody was sent to replace them.

- **Objectives:** Kill 10 Murloc Nestguards or Murloc Tidehunters, then return to Guard Pell.
  (unchanged)
- **Details:** There used to be six of us at this post. The other five were called to the city in
  spring, and nobody was sent to replace them. So when the murlocs come up from the lake at night
  and nose around the Vigil, there's only me to chase them. Kill ten of the nestguards or
  tidehunters, and I'll pay a bounty each time you come back.
- **Progress:** Have the murlocs been thinned?
- **Completion:** Ten fewer at the water's edge. They'll be back, and I'll pay for those too.

### 94506 Nests to Burn

Guard Pell, level 18 bounty. Burn 6 Murloc Nests.

Angle: Ilsa says it's cruel. Pell can't hold the shore against twice as many with one sword.

- **Objectives:** Burn 6 Murloc Nests on the lake's island, then return to Guard Pell.
  (unchanged)
- **Details:** The murlocs breed on the island, and every nest we leave means ten more by spring.
  Row out and burn six of their nests. Ilsa says it's cruel. Maybe it is. But I can't hold this
  shore against twice as many with one sword. I'll pay for each lot, and they'll have built more by
  the time you're back.
- **Progress:** Are the nests burned?
- **Completion:** Six nests gone. The island will smoke for a day. Here's your coin. There will be
  more nests.

### 94507 Riverpaw Scouts

Guard Pell, level 19 bounty. Kill 10 Riverpaw Scouts.

Angle: gnolls never came this far in when Pell was a boy.

- **Objectives:** Kill 10 Riverpaw Scouts, then return to Guard Pell. (unchanged)
- **Details:** Riverpaw gnolls have been watching the lake from the east shore. Gnolls never came
  this far in when I was a boy. Now they're everywhere, and these are scouts, and a scout who gets
  home tells his chief where to strike. Kill ten, and I'll pay a bounty, again whenever you like.
- **Progress:** Are the scouts dead?
- **Completion:** Ten scouts who won't report back. The chief will send more, and I'll pay for
  those too.

### 94508 Scout Markings

Grave-tender Ilsa, level 19 bounty. Collect 8 Riverpaw Scout Marks.

Angle: Ilsa scrubs every daub off her graves. The marks point west, to a gnoll chief gathering
the packs (Hogger).

- **Objectives:** Bring 8 Riverpaw Scout Marks to Grave-tender Ilsa. (unchanged)
- **Details:** The gnolls daub every grave they pass, and I scrub off every daub. Their scouts wear
  a token of the same paint. Bring me eight of those marks, and I'll know how many walk among my
  stones. I'll pay for each lot.
- **Progress:** Have you got the marks?
- **Completion:** The same daub on every one, and it isn't the cult's mark. It's a gnoll chief's.
  Someone out west is gathering the packs. Thank you. Bring me more if you find them.

## The Tower of Azora

### 94601 The Tower Is Open

Toren, level 19 story. Pin the three ward stones.

- **Objectives:** Pin the three ward stones of the Tower of Azora, at the lake, the foothills and
  the old tower, and report to Toren. (unchanged)
- **Details:** The master set three wards on the tower, and the cult has hung them out where I
  can't reach. One is in the shallows of the lake, one up in the Blackrock foothills, and one at
  the foot of the Ilgalar tower. A guard the cult left behind holds each one. The portals inside
  the tower lead to them, the lake's first. Kill the guard, put your hand on the stone, and the
  next portal will open.
- **Progress:** Are the wards pinned? Which are left?
- **Completion:** Three stones, three pins. I felt the tower sigh from out here. The way up is
  open. Mind the top. The master is up there, and he isn't alone.

### 94602 The Apprentice's Secret

Started by Morganth's ore cache, taken to Toren, level 21 story.

Angle: Toren and Morganth were apprentices together and made up their own alphabet so the master
couldn't read their notes. This plants the bible's cipher, which pays off in the 30s and 40s.

- **Objectives:** Take the note from Morganth's ore cache to Toren, outside the Tower of Azora.
  (unchanged)
- **Details:** The cache is lined with the same humming ore as the shard. Under the lid lies a
  note in a careful hand, signed M., Ilgalar. It says the rod in the tower's heart was never
  Theocritus's work, and that the old man is bound to the stone it stands on. Toren should read
  this.
- **Progress:** You have something from the cache?
- **Completion:** M. Ilgalar. Morganth. We were apprentices together, and we made up our own
  alphabet so the master couldn't read our notes. Then we were told he'd died, and I grieved for
  him for years. If he's alive, and he did this, then I don't know who I was grieving. I'll run
  this note to the town myself. Go on up and finish what you started.

### 94603 Theocritus Unbound

Theocritus, level 22 once. Kill the Shadow of Morganth.

- **Objectives:** Kill the Shadow of Morganth in the top chamber of the Tower of Azora, and report
  to Theocritus. (unchanged)
- **Details:** Do you see the cord? It isn't rope. I've been bound to this floor since the evening
  the rod went in, and the thing standing where my work table was has Morganth's old face and none
  of his mind. Put it down, and the cord will let go of me. Take this scroll when it does. It will
  bring you back to my door whenever you need it.
- **Progress:** It still stands. I can feel it through the cord.
- **Completion:** The cord is gone. Dear gods, the air. Take the scroll and the coin. I owe you
  more than either.

### 94604 Where It Pointed (approved)

Theocritus to Marshal Dughan, level 22 story.

- **Objectives:** Take Theocritus's letter to Marshal Dughan in Goldshire.
- **Details:** Morganth was my apprentice for eleven years. I taught him to listen to ore, and when
  he vanished, I mourned him. He set this rod, and the ore in it still hums toward wherever he went
  next. I've written down the bearing. Take this letter to Dughan, and tell him the tower was a
  signpost, not a prize.
- **Progress:** Have you seen the marshal yet?
- **Completion:** East, then, toward Redridge. I send a report to Stormwind every week, and every
  week the same answer comes back. Elwynn considered clear. Someone isn't reading them. Thank you
  for bringing this. Go and rest; you've earned it.

### 94605 Ward Shards

Toren, level 21 bounty. Collect 10 Ward Shards.

- **Objectives:** Bring 10 Ward Shards to Toren. (unchanged)
- **Details:** Whatever the cult's wards are made of, it chips off the creatures that carry them
  like glass. I can grind a chip down into something safe to hold. Bring me ten from the tower's
  gnolls, its acolytes and the voidwalkers, and I'll pay for the lot.
- **Progress:** How many shards have you got? (unchanged)
- **Completion:** Ten. That's a good handful, and the tower is a little quieter for it. Here's your
  pay. Come back with more if you can.

### 94606 Ward Shards

Theocritus, level 21 bounty. Collect 10 Ward Shards.

Angle: Theocritus's grief. He taught a boy to listen to ore and should have taught him to listen
to people.

- **Objectives:** Bring 10 Ward Shards to Theocritus. (unchanged)
- **Details:** My wards are broken glass now, and the glass is all over the floor of my tower.
  Bring me ten shards from the gnolls, the acolytes and the voidwalkers, and I'll find a use for
  them. I'll pay for the lot.
- **Progress:** How many shards have you got? (unchanged)
- **Completion:** Ten. I'll melt them down and set the wards again, properly this time. I taught a
  boy to listen to ore once. I should have taught him to listen to people. The pay is yours.
