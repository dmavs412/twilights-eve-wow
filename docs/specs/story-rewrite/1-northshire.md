# Northshire Valley (1-10)

New text for every Northshire quest, rumour note and Tier 1 Calling quest, in the voice Davis
approved in the samples. Quests marked "approved" are the samples, unchanged. Every quest keeps
its mobs, counts, items and chain. Titles are unchanged.

Northshire sits below the reveal ladder's first rung. McBride believes there was a cult in the
Abbey; nobody else does yet.

## 94001 The Idol in the Deep

Started by the Smoldering Twilight Idol, level 7 story. Bring it to Marshal McBride.

Angle: McBride saw the mark once before, wet paint on the undercroft door. He no longer names the
Twilight's Hammer, because nobody in Northshire knows that name yet.

- **Objectives:** Bring the Smoldering Twilight Idol to Marshal McBride on the steps of
  Northshire Abbey.
- **Details:** The idol is warm to the touch, though no fire burns near it. Dark wax has run down
  its sides and hardened, and something beneath it pulses like a slow heartbeat. The kobold who
  carried it wasn't digging for candles. Someone was paying him to dig. Marshal McBride, on the
  steps of the Abbey, should see this.
- **Progress:** You've found something. I can see it on your face. Out with it.
- **Completion:** I've seen that mark once before. It was painted on the undercroft door the
  morning after the Abbey fell, and the paint was still wet. Kobolds don't dig toward an abbey by
  chance. That mine runs under our feet, and whatever took the Abbey came up through the floor.

## 94002 Into the Abbey

Marshal McBride to Brother Neals, level 8 story. Find Neals inside the Abbey.

Angle: Neals hid for days. Keeps "the candles never went out," as the bible does.

- **Objectives:** Find Brother Neals inside Northshire Abbey.
- **Details:** We lost the Abbey in a single night, and until now we didn't know how. Brother Neals
  was at vespers when it fell, and no one has seen him come out. If any of the brothers lived,
  he's the one stubborn enough to have managed it. Go in through the front doors and find him.
  Then find out what's down there.
- **Progress:** - (unchanged)
- **Completion:** Light be praised, a living face! They came up through the undercroft, chanting,
  and the candles never went out. Not once, not even with the doors thrown open. I've hidden by
  this stair for days. Listen to me. Whatever they're doing below, it isn't finished.

## 94003 Padfoot's End

Marshal McBride, level 10 story. Kill Garrick Padfoot.

Angle: Padfoot ran with the Brotherhood in Westfall and was east on business when the Hush took
it. Everyone he knew is behind the grey.

- **Objectives:** Kill Garrick Padfoot in the Northshire Vineyards, then return to Marshal
  McBride.
- **Details:** The Defias in the vineyards answer to one man, Garrick Padfoot. He ran with the
  Brotherhood in Westfall, and he was east on business the week the Hush took it. Every friend he
  had is on the other side of the grey, and he's taken it out on my valley ever since. He's put
  more of my guards in the infirmary than the kobolds have. Bring him down, and you can have your
  pick of his hoard. Don't go alone if you can help it.
- **Progress:** Is Padfoot still breathing?
- **Completion:** Padfoot's dead? Then the vineyards are ours again. I almost pity him. He lost
  everything, and all he could think to do was take from people who had a little left. He kept a
  fine stash for a thief. Take whichever suits your hand. You've earned it.

## 94004 The Abbot's Fall

Brother Neals, level 10 story. Kill Abbot Ashworth.

Angle: for a year the Abbot preached that the grey was the Light's mercy. The cult let people
believe the Hush is a god, as the bible says. He taught Neals his letters.

- **Objectives:** Kill Abbot Ashworth at the top of the bell tower, then return to Brother Neals.
- **Details:** It was the Abbot who let them in. He opened the undercroft himself and stood with
  them while they chanted. For a year he preached that the grey was the Light's mercy, closing
  the world gently, like a hand over a dying man's eyes. I thought it was grief talking. Now he
  keeps the bell tower, and when he rings that bell, the brothers he turned come running. Climb
  the stair and end it. I'll pray for what's left of him.
- **Progress:** The bell. I keep hearing the bell.
- **Completion:** Then it's quiet up there at last. Light forgive him, and forgive me for being
  glad. He taught me my letters, you know. Whatever they were digging for, they'll have to dig
  without him.

## 94005 Wolves at the Fold (approved)

Eagan Peltskinner, level 3 bounty. Kill 10 Young or Timber Wolves.

- **Objectives:** Kill 10 Young Wolves or Timber Wolves in Northshire Valley, then return to Eagan
  Peltskinner.
- **Details:** There are more wolves every season, and they aren't ours. They come in thin and
  hungry from the west, out of the hills above Westfall, where my brother kept sheep before the
  Hush took it. Whatever is over there, they won't stay near it, so they come here, to my fold.
  I'll pay for every pack you thin, young or timber. It's all the same to me.
- **Progress:** Are they still out there? I haven't slept through a night in weeks.
- **Completion:** That will buy the fold a few quiet nights. More will come. They have nowhere
  else to go, the same as the rest of us. My coin will be here when they do.

## 94006 Pelts for Peltskinner (approved)

Eagan Peltskinner, level 4 bounty. Collect 8 Northshire Wolf Pelts.

- **Objectives:** Bring 8 Northshire Wolf Pelts to Eagan Peltskinner.
- **Details:** The quartermaster in Goldshire buys every pelt I send him, and his price climbs
  every month. Our furs used to come down from Dun Morogh, but that road ends in grey now, and no
  one who has walked it has come back. Either Elwynn's wolves keep the Watch warm this winter, or
  nothing does. Bring me their pelts, whole if you can manage it.
- **Progress:** Any pelts for me?
- **Completion:** Good, thick hides. That's a few more men warm at the gate this winter. Here is
  your share.

## 94007 Vermin at Echo Ridge

Deputy Willem, level 5 bounty. Kill 10 Kobold Vermin or Kobold Workers.

Angle: Willem has been a deputy for six weeks. Everyone senior went to the city when the king's
ship didn't come home. The detail is that the kobolds dig in a straight line.

- **Objectives:** Kill 10 Kobold Vermin or Kobold Workers at Echo Ridge Mine, then return to
  Deputy Willem.
- **Details:** I've been a deputy for six weeks. Everyone with more years than me went to the city
  when the king's ship didn't come home, so now it's me watching Echo Ridge. The kobolds are
  digging faster than ever, and they're digging in a straight line, toward the Abbey. Kobolds
  never dig straight. The Marshal's posted a standing bounty. Vermin or workers, they all count.
- **Progress:** Are they still digging out there?
- **Completion:** That will slow them down. It won't stop them, mind. Come back when they've dug a
  few more holes, and I'll have the bounty ready.

## 94008 Thugs in the Vineyards

Marshal McBride, level 7 bounty. Kill 10 Defias Thugs.

Angle: half the thugs picked grapes in the same rows last summer. The wine trade died with the
ships, and the Defias pay.

- **Objectives:** Kill 10 Defias Thugs in the Northshire Vineyards, then return to Marshal
  McBride.
- **Details:** Half the thugs in those vineyards picked grapes in the same rows last summer.
  There's no wine trade with the ships gone, so there are no wages, and the Defias pay. I can't
  blame a man for eating, but I can't let him rob the valley either. I can't spare the guards,
  but I can spare the coin. There's a bounty on every thug you bring down, as often as you care to
  collect it.
- **Progress:** Are the vineyards still crawling with Defias?
- **Completion:** Ten fewer thieves. Padfoot will find more. As long as there's no work, there
  will always be hungry men. My bounty will be here when he does.

## 94009 Abbey Relics

Marshal McBride, level 10 repeatable. Collect 10 Twilight Relics.

Angle: the brothers' families come to the gate every morning asking for news.

- **Objectives:** Recover 10 Twilight Relics from the Abbey's corrupted brothers and their
  masters, then return to Marshal McBride.
- **Details:** Every brother they turned carries some trinket of theirs: a wax-sealed charm, a
  carved stone, a candle that won't go out. The disciples carry more. I want all of it out of the
  Abbey and burned. The brothers' families still come to the gate every morning, asking whether
  there's news. I'd like to be able to tell them the place is clean. Bring me what you find, and
  keep bringing it.
- **Progress:** More of their filth, I hope?
- **Completion:** Into the fire with the rest. Each one we burn is one they can't use. Come back
  when the Abbey has filled up again.

## Rumour 2: The Vintner's Ledger

Overseer Maren Tolle. The ledger is the only record that forty families worked a year for casks
the Kul Tiran buyers will never pay for. The rumour's one thread is the strange coin in the
bookkeeper's purse.

### 94902 The Vintner's Ledger

Level 7 rumour quest. Collect 5 Stolen Ledger Pages from Defias Thugs.

- **Objectives:** Collect 5 Stolen Ledger Pages from Defias Thugs for Overseer Maren Tolle.
- **Details:** The Defias took the vineyard's ledger and tore it into pages, and my pickers say
  they carry the pages around with them. That ledger is the only record of what the Kul Tiran
  buyers owe us for last year's casks. They'll never pay now, not with the sea gone. But it's the
  only proof that forty families worked a full year for something. Bring back five pages.
- **Progress:** Have you found my pages?
- **Completion:** These aren't our figures. Someone has scraped ours off and written over them in
  a careful hand: wages, names and sums paid. Someone is keeping the Defias' accounts, and he's no
  thug.

### 94903 The Bookkeeper

Level 8 rumour quest. Kill the Defias Bookkeeper.

- **Objectives:** Kill the Defias Bookkeeper for Overseer Maren Tolle.
- **Details:** The pages name the man who keeps the Defias' books, and he's close. A man who writes
  that neatly was a clerk somewhere once, before the clerking dried up. That doesn't excuse him.
  Find him and put an end to his bookkeeping. Be careful. He isn't as soft as he looks.
- **Progress:** Is the bookkeeper dealt with?
- **Completion:** No more sums for the Defias. There was strange coin in his purse, too, the kind
  nobody in Northshire will change. The vineyard owes you more than I can write down.

### Notes (980033-980035, Torn Ledger Cover)

- **980033, the vineyard rows south of the Abbey:** "The Defias took my ledger and tore it to
  pages as they ran. A year of forty families' work was written in it." / "I can't prove what
  we're owed without those pages. I'll be in the vineyard rows south of the Abbey."
- **980034, the western vineyard wall:** "This cover is all that's left of the vineyard's ledger.
  The thugs have the rest." / "Bring me the pages, and I'll make it worth your while. Look for me
  at the western vineyard wall."
- **980035, the press house at the vineyard's edge:** "The press has stood idle since the ships
  stopped coming. Now the ledger is gone, too." / "Come to the press house at the vineyard's edge,
  and I'll tell you the rest."

## Rumour 3: What the Kobolds Dug Up

Prospector Hale sold every pick he owned to buy this claim. His buyers were in Ironforge, and that
road ends in grey. The rumour's one thread is the strange coin in the caches.

### 94904 What the Kobolds Dug Up

Level 7 rumour quest. Kill 8 Kobold Workers.

- **Objectives:** Kill 8 Kobold Workers for Prospector Hale.
- **Details:** I've prospected for thirty years, and I sold every pick I owned to buy this claim.
  My buyers were in Ironforge. Now the Ironforge road ends in grey, and this claim is all I have
  left. Those kobolds are digging under it and burying whatever they find. I want them stopped,
  and I want to know what they're hiding. Start with the workers.
- **Progress:** Are the workers dead?
- **Completion:** Good. With the diggers gone, I can see where they buried things.

### 94905 Unearthed Caches

Level 8 rumour quest. Use 3 Unearthed Caches.

- **Objectives:** Use 3 Unearthed Caches for Prospector Hale.
- **Details:** The workers left caches in the ground, three of them near the diggings. Open them
  and see what the kobolds thought was worth burying. I don't want what's inside. I only want to
  know.
- **Progress:** What was in the caches?
- **Completion:** Coin. Strange coin, not Stormwind's and not Ironforge's, with a foreman's
  scratch-mark on every purse. Someone is paying these kobolds, and the mark belongs to
  Grubhollow.

### 94906 Foreman Grubhollow

Level 9 rumour quest. Kill Foreman Grubhollow.

- **Objectives:** Kill Foreman Grubhollow for Prospector Hale.
- **Details:** Grubhollow runs the diggings, and he doesn't work alone. Go and put him down. His
  diggers will fight for him.
- **Progress:** Is Grubhollow dead?
- **Completion:** The claim is mine again, for whatever a claim is worth now. Take what the
  foreman guarded, and my thanks.

- **980037, the kobold diggings north of the mine:** "The kobolds have been burying caches all
