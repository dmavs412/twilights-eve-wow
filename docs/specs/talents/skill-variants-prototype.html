Warrior Skill Cards

Talent trees and talent points are gone. In their place a Basic Warrior has four talent rows, one per skill, laid out like the Mists of Pandaria panel. A row unlocks at level 2, 4, 6 or 8 and holds three cards, each a strict upgrade to that skill with no cost attached; you set one, so the choice is which strength, never a trade. An empty row can be set anywhere. A set row can only be changed at an innkeeper. The question this page tests: do four rows of three feel like progression across ten levels, and does each pick change how the kit plays? Every card shows a 30-second dummy fight at your current level with that card set, next to what you have now.

Base numbers are the real master.csv rows. The fight, the mob and the white-hit damage are placeholders, good enough to compare cards against each other, not against the game.
