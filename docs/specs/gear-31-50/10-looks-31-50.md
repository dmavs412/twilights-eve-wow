Title: Looks that follow item level for the Quest Lines, the four dungeon sets and every 31-50 plate Chest (#PARENT)
Labels: ready-for-agent

Part of #PARENT, decision 8. Blocked by 07-dungeon-epics (this spec's last earlier ticket on
`gear.csv`, `gear.toml` and `client_dbc_patch.json`), RD:20-gear-45-50 (the arc's last Quest
Line rows) and DUNGEONS:11-stonewatch-loot (the last dungeon set).

Davis's rule from the grill: looks progress with item level as in WoW, so a higher-level item
wears a higher-level vanilla look, and every band and dungeon set has its own look per armour
type. The arc's Quest Line rows took Wardrobes from low-level vanilla families, and the four
dungeon sets copied Hogger's camp rows' looks (their tickets say so). This ticket gives them new
looks by the rule, and hands out the plate looks of these levels, including this spec's own band
rows. No stats, names, icons or entries change.

## Method

- **Chests**: each row takes its own vanilla family from `python3.15
  tools/twilights-eve/gear_sheet.py looks Chest <type> <item level>`: the chest's display as
  `display_id`, the family's pieces as the eight `wardrobe_*` cells (`gear_sheet.py set`). A cloth
  Chest wears its family's robe where it has one (ADR 0030). No family any other row wears.
- **Weapons, shields, held items and bows**: each row takes a model from `gear_sheet.py looks
  <slot> <type> <item level>`: a vanilla item of the same type within 5 levels whose display no
  other row uses.
- **The icon stays.** A row whose `display_id` changes writes its Line's icon name in its `icon`
  cell if the cell is blank (the name the old display showed), so the Line keeps one icon (ADR
  0026, ADR 0030). `icon_check.py` must show no change in clashes.
- **Order.** Within one armour type, a higher item level wears a higher-level vanilla look.
- Rings and amulets keep their display: no one sees them worn.
