# Gear 31 to 50 against the Tier Step (#661)

Decision 3 of #650: gear from 31 stays on the Power Curve and does not take the Tier Step, and the
Tier 2 Tier Bonus closes the gap. The Tier Step puts x1.5 health and x1.5 damage on every creature
of level 31 and up (`content/mobs.toml` `[difficulty.tier_step]`). This report measures the gap
with the balance model at levels 33, 38, 43 and 48. It changes no data and proposes no Tier Bonus.

`docs/balance/rules.md` words its bands for levels 10 to 30. Past 30 they are applied here for
reading, not as a gate.

## How the runs were made

Run on 2026-11-09 on commit `10fd43ec` plus this ticket's change to
`tools/twilights-eve/balance/scenarios.py`: the Calling rules now run up to `TOP_LEVEL = 50`
instead of 30. Each scenario finished in under a minute, so every cell has 300 fights.

- **Run 1, as shipped**: the repo's `data/twilights-eve/` and `crates/twilight-realm/content/`.
- **Run 2, no Tier Step**: a copy of both folders under `target/agent/no-step/`, written by a
  script that sets `[difficulty.tier_step]` `health` and `damage` to 1.0 in the copy's
  `mobs.toml` and touches nothing else.

```
python3.15 tools/twilights-eve/balance/run.py --scenario pulls  --levels 33 38 43 48 --fights 300 --jobs 4 --out target/agent/runs/shipped-pulls.md
python3.15 tools/twilights-eve/balance/run.py --scenario dps    --levels 33 38 43 48 --fights 300 --jobs 4 --out target/agent/runs/shipped-dps.md
python3.15 tools/twilights-eve/balance/run.py --scenario elites --levels 33 38 43 48 --fights 300 --jobs 4 --out target/agent/runs/shipped-elites.md
python3.15 tools/twilights-eve/balance/run.py --scenario blues  --levels 33 38 43 48 --fights 300 --jobs 4 --out target/agent/runs/shipped-blues.md
python3.15 tools/twilights-eve/balance/run.py --scenario named  --levels 33 38 43 48 --fights 300 --jobs 4 --out target/agent/runs/shipped-named.md
```

Run 2 is the same five commands with `--data-root target/agent/no-step` and `nostep-` in place of
`shipped-` in each output name.

The Callings are the five with a tier of 1 or 2 in `data/twilights-eve/master.csv`. No Tier 2
Calling is in the sheet yet, so each Tier 1 Calling fights at its tier cap of 30 with the base
stats of the Power Level it stands at, as `model.calling_level` already does past 30.

Each run picks its builds against its own mobs, so a Calling's Talent Row choices can differ
between run 1 and run 2 at the same level. The kits are the same in both runs.

## Each level

The share below is the mean win of run 1 over the mean win of run 2, across the 28 cells a level
gives a verdict in (P-2, P-3, DPS, E-dmg, E-tank, B-2E and B-boss; the named bosses are apart).

**Level 33.** Run 1 fails 18 of 28 cells and run 2 fails 11. Failing in run 1 and holding in
run 2: P-2 for the Ranger and the Acolyte, P-3 for the Ranger, E-dmg for the Initiate, B-2E for
the Ranger and the Acolyte, and B-boss for the Initiate and the Acolyte. The Initiate fails P-2,
P-3, DPS and B-2E in both runs: with no Tier Step it still loses two normals 84% of the time. P-3
for the Vanguard and E-dmg for the Cutthroat and the Ranger win too often in both runs, and the
Acolyte's P-3 goes from 0% to 75%, past the top of its band. P-3 for the Cutthroat holds only
with the Tier Step (37% in run 1, 100% in run 2). Run 1 keeps 66% of run 2's win rate (56%
against 85%).

**Level 38.** Run 1 fails 20 and run 2 fails 15. Failing in run 1 and holding in run 2: P-2 for
the Acolyte, DPS for the Cutthroat and the Ranger, B-2E for the Acolyte, and B-boss for the
Initiate. The Ranger's P-2 rises from 9% to 75%, just under 80%, and its B-2E from 7% to 49%. The
Initiate fails P-2, P-3, DPS and B-2E in both runs, and its E-dmg goes from 0% to 86%, past the
band. No rule holds in run 1 and fails in run 2. Run 1 keeps 71% of run 2's win rate (59% against
83%).

**Level 43.** Run 1 fails 19 and run 2 fails 16. Failing in run 1 and holding in run 2: P-2 for
the Ranger and the Acolyte, B-2E for the Ranger, and B-boss for the Ranger, the Initiate and the
Acolyte. The Initiate fails P-2, P-3, DPS, E-dmg and B-2E in both runs. The Vanguard's P-3 goes
from 4% to 100%, from under its band to over it, and the Acolyte's B-2E from 0% to 52%. Three
rules hold only with the Tier Step: P-3 for the Cutthroat (46% to 100%), DPS for the Cutthroat
(209.7 in run 2, under the Vanguard's 218.8) and E-dmg for the Ranger (45% to 100%). Run 1 keeps
61% of run 2's win rate (49% against 80%).

**Level 48.** Run 1 fails 20 and run 2 fails 14. Failing in run 1 and holding in run 2: P-2 for
the Ranger and the Acolyte, P-3 for the Acolyte, E-dmg for the Initiate, B-2E for the Ranger, and
B-boss for the Ranger, the Initiate and the Acolyte. The Initiate fails P-2, P-3, DPS and B-2E in
both runs, and the Acolyte's B-2E rises from 0% to 79%, one point short. The Ranger's E-dmg goes
from 31% to 100%. P-3 and DPS for the Cutthroat hold only with the Tier Step. Run 1 keeps 54% of
run 2's win rate (43% against 80%). The share falls from 66% at 33 to 54% at 48: the gap the Tier
Bonus has to close grows with level.

Two rules fail in both runs at every level for reasons the Tier Step does not explain:

- E-tank: in run 2 the Cutthroat and the Ranger win every elite fight too, so no tank can win
  more than every damage Calling. In run 1 the Vanguard wins 95 to 99% but the Cutthroat matches
  or beats it at every level, and the Acolyte wins 53%, 76%, 45% and 4% at the four levels.
- The Initiate: it fails P-2 in run 2 at every level (8 to 35%) and B-2E at 0 to 1%. Its DPS
  against one normal (110 to 164) stays under both tanks in both runs.

The Ranger's P-2 cell at 38 in run 2 is an outlier: 87 DPS against two normals, against 144 at 33
and 158 at 43, while its DPS cell at 38 is the highest of any Calling (272). It is worth a look
before the fit.
